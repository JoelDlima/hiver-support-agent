# Interim — golden-set adjudication and agreement gate (depth investigation)

Locus: golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate — What agreement metric thresholds and adjudication rules certify the 150-250 golden set before any headline metric is quoted?
Corpus tag: hiver-support-agent-audit-6d951f. Sources read in full via `note show -j`: the five assigned notes below. Additional fetches used: 0/5 (corpus saturated on machinery + gates; remaining uncertainty is a design decision, not a literature gap).

## 1. What each source contributes (label-type → metric → gate)

### 1a. Brenndoerfer (Language AI Handbook, Mar 2026) — [[cohen-fleiss-krippendorff-iaa-metrics-implementation-interactive]]
- Core machinery: Cohen's kappa = (Po-Pe)/(1-Pe), pair of raters, chance-corrected; Fleiss generalizes to N raters under interchangeability (same rater count per item, pooled marginals); Krippendorff's alpha = 1-Do/De, any rater count, missing data, any scale via distance metric (nominal = binary disagreement; ordinal/interval = weighted distance).
- Metric-selection rules load-bearing for this locus: standard (unweighted) kappa treats all disagreements equally — correct for nominal intent labels, wrong for ordered reply-quality scales; weighted kappa (linear vs quadratic) gives partial credit to near-misses — quadratic penalizes large gaps disproportionately and approximates ICC under assumptions, preferred when large errors are harmful (e.g., rating a dangerous reply 5/5 vs 1/5). Treating ordinal as nominal underestimates agreement; treating nominal as ordinal overstates it — the distance-metric choice must be declared.
- Gates: Landis & Koch (1977) bands (0.00-0.20 slight, 0.21-0.40 fair, 0.41-0.60 moderate, 0.61-0.80 substantial, 0.81-1.00 almost perfect) are reported but explicitly warned against mechanical use: kappa depends on prevalence and task difficulty (0.6 excellent for sarcasm/pragmatics, poor for factual entity work where >0.9 expected; benchmarks came from medicine, do not auto-transfer to NLP). Principled alternative endorsed: set task-specific thresholds before annotation from application risk (high-stakes >0.8; exploratory >0.5 + disagreement analysis).
- Prevalence warning: high raw agreement with skewed marginals can still yield low kappa (and vice versa) — must report per-label breakdowns and marginals, not a single number.

### 1b. Koji docs (2026) — [[data-annotation-quality-guidelines-agreement-metrics-gold-tasks-2026]]
- Quality = four separable systems: guidelines (decision procedure, not glossary) + agreement metric (is task learnable?) + gold tasks (is annotator calibrated today?) + adjudication (what turns disagreement into a guideline change). Fixing one does not fix others.
- Metric default: Krippendorff's alpha as production default (survives missing labels, reassignment, attrition; Cohen/Fleiss assume tidy matrices). Conventional gates stated crisply: alpha >= 0.800 reliable ground truth; 0.667-0.800 tentative conclusions only, do not ship as eval set; < 0.667 specification broken — rewrite guideline, do not hire more annotators. Cohen bands repeated (0.61-0.80 substantial, 0.81-1.00 almost perfect) with subjectivity calibration (0.65 on safety may be excellent; 0.75 on mechanical task is a red flag). Always report per-label agreement: overall 0.82 can hide one critical label at 0.31.
- Gold tasks: inject 3-10% rotating honeypots, ~90% rolling-accuracy blocking threshold (<3% detects drift too slowly; >10% taxes throughput). Static honeypots leak (fastest recognizers are the cheaters). Load-bearing limitation: gold items are by construction the adjudicable-easy cases, so gold accuracy systematically over-reports quality on the hard slice. Fix: parallel disagreement audit — stratified weekly sample of split items, senior-reviewer adjudicated. Gold = compliance; disagreement audit = well-formedness.
- Adjudication: majority vote is "the single largest source of quiet quality loss" (cites Aroyo & Welty CrowdTruth: disagreement is signal; Plank on label variation: majority encodes bias against minority perspectives). Policy: route-don't-average (disagreements → senior reviewer, not vote); reviewer writes one-sentence why → guideline changelog with date (revisions version the labels); keep full distribution for subjective tasks (6/4 ≠ 10/0); escalate patterns not items (same boundary 3 weeks → revise guideline + re-annotate slice). Floor: 3 annotators for judgment tasks (detect disagreement at all); 5+ for subjective where distribution is data. Snow et al. 2008 cited: specification, not expertise, is usually the bottleneck.

### 1c. Datavlab/Andraos (Aug 2026) — [[inter-annotator-agreement-for-llm-evaluation-guide]]
- Targets calibrated to subjectivity (most actionable gate table in corpus): objective tasks (fact presence, syntax) 0.90+; moderately subjective (topic/content categorization, summary completeness, coarse quality) 0.70-0.85; inherently subjective (preference, tone, fine-grained helpfulness/harm) 0.60-0.75 — with Twitter sarcasm cited at alpha often < 0.35 and sometimes negative, which reflects task subjectivity, not annotator failure. Intent classification for support tweets sits in the middle band; golden-eval certification must still clear the higher bar (see position).
- Metric map: Cohen = two-annotator categorical (intent pilot); Fleiss = multi-annotator pilot with same coverage (guideline calibration); alpha = production scale incl. ordinal 1-5 quality ratings (must declare nominal vs ordinal vs interval); correlation family for continuous/ranked: Pearson/Spearman for score consistency, Kendall's tau for preference ordering, Bradley-Terry for RLHF-style preference. Warns correlation ≠ agreement (perfect correlation with systematic offset possible).
- Operations: 5-15% structured overlap with 2-3 annotations for IAA monitoring (random-within-strata, not easy-only); rolling-window computation with alerts (gradual = fatigue/drift; sudden = new annotator or edge case; subcategory-localized = guideline gap); intervention matched to cause (individual retrain vs guideline + examples vs group calibration). False-precision warning: 95% CI on alpha spans ~0.05-0.10 even with hundreds of items — treat scores as bands, not four-decimal rankings. IAA shows reliability, and high IAA can mask shared misunderstanding ("surface agreement, deep disagreement" — periodic why-reviews needed).

### 1d. James (Sheffield, arXiv Apr 2026) — [[counting-on-consensus-selecting-the-right-inter-annotator-agreement-metric-for-n]]
- Scholarly anchor: organizes metrics by task type; stresses chance-model assumptions differ so scores are not comparable across metrics; class imbalance, rater count, granularity all shift interpretation. Endorses: weighted kappa for Likert/ordinal (linear vs quadratic declared); Krippendorff for mixed designs; Gwet AC1/AC2 as prevalence-robust supplement when kappa collapses under extreme imbalance (recent NLP practice: report AC1 alongside kappa); ICC for continuous (two-way random, absolute agreement), CCC when mean/scale offset matters, Spearman/Kendall for rank-order IAA and human-model ranking studies.
- Reporting norms (aligns with Hallgren 2012; Hayes & Krippendorff 2007; Klie et al. 2024 meta-analysis of 591 datasets): always report CIs (bootstrap), rater design, missing-data handling, prevalence, annotator expertise; p-vs-chance is not a substitute for CI magnitude/precision. Fixed Landis/Koch–style cutoffs "overly rigid" for complex/subjective tasks (Wong et al. 2021: replication-based benchmarks instead); Klie et al.: most papers ignore imbalance/sample-size/expertise — transparency required. Reliability ≠ validity (Artstein & Poesio 2008): high IAA can come from oversimplified/biased guidelines; pair coefficients with validity evidence (ambiguous-case examples, external reference). Disagreement handling: retain soft labels/distributions, report dispersion alongside coefficients, analyze systematic class/subgroup disagreement, document training/interface effects; rater-aware modeling as advanced option.

### 1e. Olumide/MachineLearningMastery (Jul 2026) — [[llm-evaluation-frameworks-compared-how-to-actually-measure-what-your-model-does]]
- Judge-trust evidence: LLM-as-judge (used by RAGAS/DeepEval/Promptfoo) carries three measured biases — position bias (IJCNLP 2025: 15 judges × ~150k instances; swapping order flips verdicts; 10-15% flip rates common in production, enough to corrupt borderline pass/fail), self-preference (same-family judge inflates own outputs; harmful form = failure to penalize own errors), verbosity (longer rated higher regardless of content). The "80% human agreement" figure is MT-Bench aggregate, not a per-task production guarantee.
- Prescribed mitigations (adopted into position): swap-order audit harness (run both orderings, report flip rate; average or dual-call), cross-family judge (judge model family ≠ generator family), verbosity control (length-normalized or explicit brevity instruction + length-stratified analysis), CI-gated thresholds (e.g., 0.7 pass tuned to risk). Stack advice (RAGAS faithfulness + DeepEval CI gating + Promptfoo multi-model/red-team; two-tool pattern) is out of this locus's scope except that faithfulness-style claim-support checks belong to the reply-groundedness rubric owned by the caged-judge locus.

## 2. Synthesis for the golden set (what the corpus agrees on vs where I set the gate)

- Agreement on machinery, disagreement on numbers — exactly as the locus rationale states. All sources converge on: nominal → kappa/alpha; ordinal → weighted kappa / ordinal alpha; continuous/ranked → ICC/CCC/Spearman/Kendall; report CIs + per-label + prevalence; adjudicate-by-expert, never bare majority; gold tasks + disagreement audits as complementary. They diverge on the certifying number (0.667 vs 0.70 vs 0.80 vs 0.90), because the right number is task- and risk-dependent. The locus answer is therefore a tiered gate, not a single cutoff.
- Placement of this golden set: intent labels are nominal + moderately subjective (Twitter noise, sarcasm, multi-intent) → human-human bar in the 0.70-0.85 band per Datavlab, but an eval set quoted as ground truth must clear Krippendorff's 0.80 reliability line per Koji/Hayes (0.667-0.80 = tentative only). Reply-quality/escalation ratings are ordinal/binary-imbalanced → weighted-kappa/ordinal-alpha + correlation + prevalence-aware supplements. Twitter sarcasm stratum is expected to underperform (<0.35 possible per Datavlab) — report it separately; do not let it veto or inflate the global gate.
- Sampling consensus: random-within-strata overlap (Datavlab 5-15%), never easy-only; gold 3-10% rotating with ~90% block (Koji); full double-annotation is affordable at n=200 so the golden set itself is 100% overlapped (stronger than production monitoring); disagreement-distribution audit mandatory because gold over-samples easy cases. Confidence ratings 1-5 per item (Koji) surface unanimous-but-uncertain items gold never catches.
- Judge-human agreement: corpus treats human-human IAA as prerequisite and judge-human as a second, weaker, bias-prone measurement requiring its own metrics (correlation + weighted kappa + rank correlation) and its own bias audits — never a single "80% agree" claim. This locus therefore gates headlines on human-human first, judge-human second.

## 3. Gaps and limits
- No source gives a Twitter-support-specific sampling fraction or an n=200 power calculation; the n and strata below are my design synthesis from CI-width facts (0.05-0.10 at hundreds of items) and assignment constraints (150-250, intent/difficulty/escalation/temporal coverage), not a quoted prescription. Flagged as such in the report.
- Prevalence-robust supplements (Gwet AC1, PABAK) are endorsed but not given sharp gates in the corpus — I position them as diagnostic companions, not pass/fail criteria.
- Correlation-vs-agreement tension (James: correlation is association, not reliability) is resolved by requiring both: weighted-kappa/alpha for absolute agreement AND Spearman/Kendall for ranking fidelity on the judge protocol.

## Committed position
1. Golden-set spec — n=200 hand-labelled threads (within the mandated 150-250), conversation-disjoint from all train/retrieval pools and drawn from the pre-cutoff period (leakage rule owned jointly with the temporal-split locus): 120 randomly sampled within strata + 80 targeted hard cases (short/ambiguous, multi-intent, sarcasm/negation, identifier-heavy order-number tweets, context-dependent multi-turn). Strata with minimums: intent (6-7 intents, >=20 per intent, rare intents oversampled); decision (60-80 escalate / rest auto-handle, so both classes have stable estimates); difficulty (the 120/80 random/hard split); temporal (span the full brand timeline plus a held recent-month slice for drift); every item carries all three labels (intent + reference resolution note + escalate/auto-handle with reason). Labelling instructions are a one-page ordered decision procedure (request? → intent → resolvable-from-history? → escalate-rule) plus an edge-case catalog with 2-3 examples per boundary, confidence 1-5 per label, and a pilot of 30 items whose disagreements revise the guideline before main labelling; all 200 items get 2-3 independent labels (100% overlap — affordable at this n).
2. Agreement gates (block headlines until passed) — intent (nominal): Krippendorff nominal alpha primary, Cohen pairwise + Fleiss pilot as secondary; SHIP only if overall alpha >= 0.80 with 95% bootstrap CI lower bound >= 0.70, per-label alpha >= 0.70 and no label < 0.60 (plus Gwet AC1 reported when a label is rarer than ~10%); reply quality (ordinal 1-5): quadratic-weighted kappa + Krippendorff ordinal alpha >= 0.70 with Spearman >= 0.70; escalation (binary, imbalanced): kappa >= 0.70 plus F1 vs adjudicated gold with prevalence disclosed; 0.667-0.80 overall = tentative (fix guideline, re-annotate affected slice, re-measure — do not quote headlines); < 0.667 = block, rewrite spec. The Twitter-sarcasm hard slice is reported separately and never averaged away.
3. Adjudication + judge-agreement protocol — no bare majority votes: every disagreement routes to a named senior reviewer who records a one-sentence why that becomes a dated guideline-changelog entry (labels versioned pre/post revision; same boundary recurring 3x triggers revision + slice re-annotation; full soft-label distribution preserved for subjective items); rotating 3-10% gold honeypots with ~90% rolling block run during labelling for compliance, plus a mandatory disagreement-distribution audit (gold measures compliance, disagreement measures well-formedness). Judge-human certification runs only after human-human gates pass, on all 200 items with a cross-family judge: require quadratic-weighted kappa >= 0.65-0.70 AND Spearman/Kendall >= 0.70, position-swap flip rate < 10%, and explicit verbosity/self-preference checks with a per-dimension disagreement table + 5-10 qualitative failure examples; the MT-Bench "80% agree" aggregate is cited as context, never as a pass criterion.
4. Confidence: 0.75 (high on metric→label-type mapping and adjudication rule where the corpus converges; moderate on the exact 0.80/0.70 numbers, which are risk-calibrated design choices rather than theorems). What would change my mind: (a) a replication on noisy Twitter support showing headline system rankings stable despite alpha in the 0.667-0.80 band (would downgrade the 0.80 block to a warning); (b) evidence that quadratic-weighted kappa systematically overstates judge reliability relative to rank metrics on groundedness (would promote Kendall/Spearman to primary); (c) a power/CI demonstration that n=150 holds per-label CI width under ~0.10 across 7 intents (would accept the cheaper n).
