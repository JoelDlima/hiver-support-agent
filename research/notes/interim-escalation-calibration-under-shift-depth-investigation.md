## 1. Dialectic: learned calibrator vs MaxProb floor

Pro-calibrator pole: Varshney et al. 2020/2022-Repl4NLP trains a calibrator on held-out instances annotated by correctness likelihood from two signals — model prediction confidence (positively correlated with correctness) and model-based difficulty score (negatively correlated) — and uses it as confidence estimator g-tilde. Reported AUC-of-risk-coverage gains over MaxProb of (15.81% IID, 5.64% OOD) on NLI and (6.19% IID, 13.9% OOD) on duplicate detection [[towards-improving-selective-prediction-ability-of-nlp-systems]]. Xin et al. 2021 (Art of Abstention) similarly finds headroom over raw MaxProb via error regularization and that pre-trained transformers improve both accuracy and confidence quality simultaneously [[the-art-of-abstention-selective-prediction-and-error-regularization-for-natural]]. Whitehead et al. 2022 shows a learned multimodal selector more than doubles coverage at 1% risk (6.8% -> 16.3%, 2.4x) on VQA v2 despite 71%+ base accuracy, and that post-hoc calibration alone often beats raw softmax [[reliable-visual-question-answeringabstain-rather-than-answer-incorrectly]].

Anti-calibrator pole (same author group, larger test): Varshney et al. Findings 2022 evaluates 17 datasets across IID/OOD/adversarial and finds NONE of the existing approaches (calibrators, MCD, label smoothing) consistently and considerably outperforms MaxProb in all three settings despite extra held-out data/compute; MCD wins duplicate detection but fails NLI especially OOD; gains do not transfer across tasks [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]]. Convergent survey synthesis: at very high target coverage softmax-thresholding is on par with elaborate selectors; only under aggressive abstention do integrated methods (SelectiveNet, SAT+ensemble, conformal rejection) further reduce risk; OOD/adversarial robustness remains open, all methods suffer coverage drops or risk inflation under severe shift [[selective-prediction-tasks]]; standard selectors do not consistently order each other across IID/OOD/ADV [[selective-prediction-in-ai]]. Gu & Hopkins 2023 adds a methodological warning: naive AURC comparisons confound accuracy and ranking; their `refinement` metric isolates confidence-function quality — many claimed wins vanish once calibrated [[on-the-evaluation-of-neural-selective-prediction-methods-for-natural-language-pr]].

Resolution: calibrator wins are real but task-conditional, not universal. The Hiver policy must therefore treat temperature-scaled MaxProb as the robustness floor and admit a learned calibrator only on proof of cross-split AURC/refinement wins. This matches the run's evidence norm (measured experiments outrank claims) and the locus rationale (floor vs learned calibrator sets the policy chapter).

## 2. Confidence signal: MaxProb + temperature scaling as floor; calibrator as gated upgrade

Guo et al. 2017 establishes why raw softmax cannot be trusted directly: modern deep nets (ResNet vs LeNet on CIFAR-100) are systematically overconfident — mean confidence substantially exceeds accuracy — and a single-parameter temperature scaling (Platt variant, fit on held-out NLL) is surprisingly the most effective post-hoc fix on most vision and document-classification sets [[supplementary-materials-foron-calibration-of-modern-neural-networks]]. ReCoVERR's calibration study reinforces the procedure: compare candidate confidences (token-product, token-mean, self-prompt P(yes)) by calibration error on a held-out slice (their 5k A-OKVQA-train), Platt-scale the winner on a disjoint slice (their remaining 12k), then deploy only the winner (self-prompt for BLIP family, token-mean for LLaVA) [[selective-selective-prediction-reducing-unnecessary-abstention-in-vision-languag]].

Critical nuance from 2026 surveys: monotone calibration (temperature/Platt) cannot repair ranking error — it rescales but does not reorder — so it improves ECE and threshold interpretability but not AURC; closing AURC requires improving the ordering of uncertainty scores (Rabanser 2025, cited in [[selective-prediction-tasks]]). Hence the two-layer signal design: (a) always temperature-scale for interpretable thresholds; (b) measure ranking separately via AURC/refinement before claiming any learned signal beats MaxProb.

For Hiver intent classification (small brand-derived taxonomy, noisy short tweets): primary signal s(x) = temperature-scaled MaxProb of the intent head. Gated upgrade: logistic/GBM calibrator on held-out features [top-2 softmax gap (margin), entropy, utterance length, retrieval similarity of nearest brand exemplar, difficulty proxy such as train-epoch variance] — exactly the Varshney-2020 recipe (confidence + difficulty) — accepted only if it beats scaled-MaxProb on AURC AND refinement on both IID-held-out and temporal-held-out (see section 3). For the reply-generator stage, a second signal is required because intent confidence does not cover grounding: NLI entailment P(premise=retrieved brand exemplars entails hypothesis=drafted reply) with threshold pi_NLImin (ReCoVERR uses 0.9) gates auto-handle independently — low entailment escalates even when intent is confident.

## 3. Threshold procedure on a held-out calibration split (with finite-sample honesty)

Toolkit operationalization [[github-ercedutselective-prediction-toolkit-github]]: sort calibration samples by s(x) descending, sweep tau, compute coverage C(tau) = fraction accepted and selective risk R(tau) = error on accepted; AURC = trapezoidal area over C in (0,1]; policy wrapper picks tau as the score quantile matching a target on calibration, then measures achieved coverage/risk on test. ReCoVERR's deployment rule is the risk-targeted dual: given application risk tolerance r, choose gamma@r = argmax coverage s.t. R<=r on calibration, then apply to test [[selective-selective-prediction-reducing-unnecessary-abstention-in-vision-languag]].

Conformal grounding and its limit: with n fresh i.i.d. calibration points, split-conformal gives 1-alpha <= P(Y in T) <= 1-alpha+1/(n+1) without model or distributional assumptions [[a-gentle-introduction-to-conformal-prediction-and-distribution-free-uncertainty]]. For Hiver this justifies the calibration-split ritual (n>=500 fresh, conversation-disjoint) but the i.i.d./exchangeability premise FAILS under the assignment's own shift (brand drift over time, new issue spikes, Twitter noise). Therefore: (i) calibration split must be conversation-disjoint AND temporal (last time window, e.g. most recent 20% of brand threads) — never random-stratified alone; (ii) select tau by risk target on the temporal calibration slice; (iii) report calibration->test generalization gap explicitly (coverage and risk at tau on IID-test vs temporal-test); (iv) never quote a conformal-style guarantee without the exchangeability disclaimer — the mandatory "What is misleading about my headline number?" section must state that any coverage-at-risk claim is conditional on no further shift.

Concrete Hiver procedure: freeze intent model; fit temperature T on cal-fit half (NLL); on cal-select half compute risk-coverage curve for scaled-MaxProb (and challenger calibrator); fix operating risk r (recommend r=5% intent error on auto-handled, with sensitivity at 1% and 10% mirroring Whitehead/ReCoVERR reporting); set tau = gamma@r; lock tau; evaluate once on golden 150-250 (which must itself be temporal + conversation-disjoint). Report: AURC, AUGRC(gamma=0.5), selective risk at coverages {70%,90%}, coverage at risks {1%,5%,10%}, Effective Reliability Phi_1 (section 4), and tau-generalization gap. Any tau re-tuning on golden invalidates the headline — golden is test-only.

## 4. Over-abstention cost: the dominant failure mode at low risk tolerance

Three independent quantifications agree that naive low-risk thresholding collapses utility:
- Whitehead: SOTA VQA 71%+ accuracy but <8% coverage at 1% risk with vanilla MaxProb; learned selector 6.8%->16.3% (2.4x) only partly closes the gap to the oracle [[reliable-visual-question-answeringabstain-rather-than-answer-incorrectly]].
- ReCoVERR: BLIP2 at 10% risk answers just ~4% of A-OKVQA; 94% of correct predictions abstained on; evidence-recovery raises coverage up to +20pp and recall +25-30% without raising risk at 20% tolerance, but at strict 10% tolerance both ReCoVERR and vision-tools overshoot risk by 1-4pp — recovery is not free [[selective-selective-prediction-reducing-unnecessary-abstention-in-vision-languag]].
- Post-abstention (11 QA sets): selective 70%-acc@100%-cov -> 85%-acc@71%-cov, then re-attempting abstained lifts to ~80% coverage without considerable accuracy drop; overall risk improvements up to 21.81 IID / 24.23 OOD [[post-abstention-towards-reliably-re-attempting-the-abstained-instances-in-qa]].

Metric that prices abstention: Effective Reliability Phi_c (Whitehead) scores +1 correct, -c incorrect, 0 abstain; Phi_1 used throughout ReCoVERR tables alongside risk/coverage/recall R_SP (fraction of dataset correct answers actually answered) [[reliable-visual-question-answeringabstain-rather-than-answer-incorrectly]][[selective-selective-prediction-reducing-unnecessary-abstention-in-vision-languag]]. Toolkit generalizes the same idea as AUGRC: GRC(c)=risk(c)+gamma*(1-c), area under it penalizes low coverage with weight gamma (default 0.5) [[github-ercedutselective-prediction-toolkit-github]].

Hiver implication: the report must never quote risk alone — always risk-coverage pairs plus Phi_1 and AUGRC(0.5) plus R_SP plus "% correct abstained on" (the ReCoVERR 94% statistic is the template). Operationally, insert a post-abstention second chance BEFORE final escalate: on first-threshold abstain, re-attempt once via retrieval re-query (hybrid re-rank top-k with cross-encoder) and/or paraphrase-re-ask, accept only if recovered confidence clears a second, pre-registered bar (entailment >= pi_NLImin); log recovery rate and its marginal risk. This converts pure abstention cost into a measured recovery win, exactly the Varshney-post-abstention/ReCoVERR pattern, and gives the decision log a non-obvious entry competitors will lack.

## 5. Multi-signal escalation reasons beyond confidence (graded deliverable)

The assignment grades decision+reason, and confidence alone cannot produce reasons. Corpus-convergent reason taxonomy:
- low_intent_confidence (s(x)<tau; include margin/entropy values)
- grounding_fail (retrieved exemplars below similarity floor or NLI entailment < pi_NLImin; cite ReCoVERR reliable+relevant evidence filter)
- sensitive_action_or_PII (refund/account change, financial/health identifiers, repeated-refusal or injection suspicion — escalate per Cobbai prompt-safety paths: exceeds-AI-capability, complex/sensitive, ambiguous, compliance-risk, repeated refusals [[safety-and-pii-in-customer-support-redaction-refusals-and-escalation-paths-for-p]])
- ambiguity_or_missing_slot (multi-intent, missing order/account detail needed for safe action)
- novelty_OOD (distance-to-training / new-cluster flag; brand issue spike)
Presentation matters: informing humans of deferral status WITHOUT surfacing the model's uncertain guess improves downstream human accuracy, whereas revealing the low-confidence guess degrades it (Bondi et al. 2021, via [[selective-prediction-tasks]] and [[selective-prediction-in-ai]]) — so the escalated handoff UI should show reason code + evidence, not the suppressed draft, unless a human explicitly requests it.

## 6. What this means for the untrusted workspace audit

Any existing implementation that (a) thresholds raw unscaled softmax, (b) tunes tau on the golden/test set, (c) reports accuracy or risk without coverage/AURC/AUGRC/Phi_1, (d) uses a single confidence number with no reason taxonomy, or (e) ignores temporal shift, is untrustworthy by construction under the above evidence — flag each as a numbered audit finding with the cited statistic (e.g. Guo miscalibration, Varshney-2022 non-dominance, ReCoVERR 94%-correct-abstained, toolkit same-accuracy-different-AURC trap).

## Committed position

Escalation policy: primary signal temperature-scaled intent MaxProb (T fit on cal-fit half) ANDed with an NLI grounding-entailment gate (pi_NLImin=0.9, pre-registered; low entailment always escalates as grounding_fail regardless of intent confidence); a learned confidence+difficulty calibrator is admitted only if it beats scaled-MaxProb on both AURC and refinement on IID-held-out AND temporal-held-out, otherwise MaxProb stands as the robustness floor per Varshney-2022. Threshold procedure: conversation-disjoint temporal calibration split (n>=500; T on cal-fit, tau=gamma@r on cal-select with operating risk r=5% intent error on auto-handled plus 1%/10% sensitivity), tau locked before touching the 150-250 golden set, exchangeability explicitly disclaimed in the headline-critique section. Reason taxonomy (every escalate logs code + signal values): low_intent_confidence, grounding_fail, sensitive_action_or_PII, ambiguity_or_missing_slot, novelty_OOD; handoff shows reason + evidence, not the suppressed draft. Reporting: full risk-coverage curve with AURC, AUGRC(gamma=0.5), selective risk at 70%/90% coverage, coverage at 1%/5%/10% risk, Phi_1 (c=1), R_SP, %correct-abstained, post-abstention recovery rate with marginal risk, and calibration->test generalization gap; a one-stage retrieval re-attempt precedes final escalate. Confidence: 7/10 — floor and procedure are strongly evidenced; exact r=5% and pi_NLImin=0.9 are brand-data-dependent and must be re-fit. What would change my mind: a challenger calibrator showing consistent, considerable AURC+refinement wins over scaled-MaxProb across IID, temporal, and brand-shift slices (not one split), or evidence that per-class/subgroup thresholds beat a global tau on Phi_1 without inflating tail risk, or a demonstration that surfacing suppressed drafts to handlers beats defer-only handoff on this brand's data.
