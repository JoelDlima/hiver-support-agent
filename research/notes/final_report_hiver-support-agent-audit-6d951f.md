# Hiver Support Agent: Forensic Audit and Trustworthy Re-engineering Plan for One Brand on Customer Support on Twitter

**Existing workspace headline numbers are uninterpolated upper bounds** until a thread-aware temporal rebuild with retrieval exclusion is completed. On that rebuilt foundation, trust per complexity is maximized by a plain dependency-ordered pipeline — conditional SetFit-first routing, gated hybrid retrieval, a caged pointwise judge with tiered gates, and a scaled MaxProb escalation floor scored by AUGRC — where every complexity upgrade ships only behind a pre-registered brand-data measurement with explicit reversal conditions.

The assignment picks one brand from Customer Support on Twitter, builds intent classification plus grounded reply generation plus an auto-handle versus escalate decider, and proves trustworthiness with a 150 to 250 item golden set, an evaluation harness, LLM-as-judge plus human agreement, two baselines, a concise report, and a decision log — spec per the task brief. The proof is the product. The system here is deliberately minimal. Every added stage must earn its place on rebuilt brand data within a 15 minute reproduction budget.

## 1. Problem Framing — What Good and Trustworthy Mean for One Brand

Good for one brand means three verifiable behaviors on future messages. The intent label routes to the correct handling path. The drafted reply is entailed by how that brand actually resolved similar threads. The auto-handle versus escalate call matches what a careful handler would do, with a stated reason every time. Trustworthy adds the proof layer. Those behaviors are measured on a conversation-disjoint temporally posterior golden set of 150 to 250 hand-labelled threads, scored against two honest baselines, with an LLM judge whose agreement with humans is itself measured and gated before any headline is quoted.

The scope is narrow by design. One brand means one issue distribution, one voice, one resolution playbook. Trust evidence at n equals 200 has statistical meaning only inside that scope. Averaging across a retailer and a device maker and an airline can look adequate while being unsafe on each, and per-label agreement math at n equals 200 already forces a small taxonomy with no headroom for cross-brand variation.

The agent classifies, drafts, and defers. It does not execute actions, issue refunds, change accounts, or call tools. Any sensitive action, missing slot, or low-confidence grounding escalates with a reason code. The handoff default shows reason and evidence while suppressing the uncertain draft unless a handler explicitly requests it — a default hypothesis pending a handler A/B on golden escalations (accuracy + time-on-task + override rate); show-draft is reinstated only on an accuracy-neutral plus time win.

Four contracts make the claim testable:

- **Intent** — 6 to 8 distinguishable intents — an author design ceiling from 200-item/20-per-intent arithmetic — route correctly on future threads with graceful out-of-scope handling.
- **Generation** — every actionable claim is entailed by cited brand history, the next step matches brand playbook, and no policy or compensation or timeline or personal data is invented.
- **Escalation** — the auto-handled slice meets a pre-registered risk target and every escalation carries a coded reason plus evidence.
- **Reproducibility** — a stranger reproduces the headline in under 15 minutes from a frozen subsample — an author repro target, motivated by replicability risk [[if-you-build-your-own-ner-scorer-non-replicable-results-will-come-acl-anthology]].

Deliberately not built, each with revival in parentheses:

- multi-brand generality (revisit only on per-brand gold at n equals 200 each);
- live channel integration (revisit only post-gold gates);
- multi-step tool loops (revisit only on a second retrieve-grade-retry loop);
- learned sparse retrieval (revisit only as document-only SPLADE candidacy on the 5-point identifier gate);
- dynamic fusion weights (revisit only after 40-plus relevance pairs with alpha-sweep win);
- fine-tuned generation or judging, including DSPy prompt programs (revisit only on groundedness/agreement delta on brand gold);
- streaming evaluation (revisit only on production drift monitoring);
- full 3M-row scale claims (revisit only on embedded-to-Postgres migration with p50/p95 ledger). Each exclusion bets that complexity buys less trust than the same hours spent on adjudication and leakage control. Deployment on new threads is out-of-distribution by definition [[211011334-generalized-out-of-distribution-detection-a-survey]][[beyond-the-known-investigating-llms-performance-on-out-of-domain-intent-detectio]], and interpolating-split metrics do not license deployment claims.

## 2. Data Foundation — Conversation Graph, Brand Selection, Splits, and Leakage Control

Raw Twitter support data is not independent tweets. Each row carries tweet identifier, anonymized author, inbound flag, timestamp, masked text, plus reply linkage that reconstructs threads [[customer-support-on-twitter-openbigdataorg]], widely mirrored as the approximately 3M-row thoughtvector source [[github-amruta33customer-support-on-twitter-the-customer-support-on-twitter-datas]]. Any step that flattens threads to pairs removes the grouping key evaluation must respect.

The derivative choice proves the point. The TNE-AI build preserves threading across 794,000 rows and 109 companies [[tne-aicustomer-support-on-twitter-conversation-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. The 945,000-row input-output derivative distills threads into normalized pairs with slang and emoji and URL cleaning [[mohammadothmanmo-customer-support-tweets-945k-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. The latter suits fine-tuning and is unusable for leakage auditing because the thread identifier is gone. Never adopt a pair-flattened derivative as the primary source. Rebuild from raw data and carry identifiers forward.

Brand choice maximizes submission quality, not row count. Exact-corpus analysis reports AmazonHelp at 169,840 tweets and 168,823 responses with median 11.47 minutes, AppleSupport at 106,860 and 106,648 with median 70.97 minutes, and TMobileHelp at 34,317 and 34,229 with median 2.75 minutes. AmericanAir is at 36,764 with median 10.73 minutes, and British Airways at 29,361 with median 180.5 minutes [[github-abh2050customer_support_intelligence-an-interactive-streamlit-app-leverag]]. Structured high-volume playbooks are inferred to support grounding while slow sparse responders undermine it — author inference from brand volume/latency stats, not a source finding [[github-abh2050customer_support_intelligence-an-interactive-streamlit-app-leverag]]. Corpus precedents cover count-vectorizer through FastText exploration [[github-jessica1438twitter-customer-support-dataset-textual-analysis-and-perspect]].

Forensics on the current workspace are specific.

The headline inbound pool holds 97,592 rows without thread or time keys, so disjoint or temporal splitting is impossible from that artifact. The 200-row golden file carries text plus labels without tweet or thread or time keys, so provenance is untraceable and overlap with train and retrieval pools is unauditable. All 200 golden texts match pool texts verbatim. The 89,694-row knowledge base is outbound with identifiers preserved, so it structurally holds gold responses while same-thread containment cannot be checked for lack of keys — the retriever fetching the exact gold answer with the judge rewarding groundedness is unmeasurable, which is itself the finding. A thread-preserving artifact with thread and timestamp and position columns exists elsewhere in the repo but was not applied to the headline brand.

Three mechanisms are live:

- Same-thread overlap shares entities and resolution state across turns.
- Target-response retrieval puts gold answers in the index.
- Future-answering-past trains on later policy to answer earlier queries, violating the requirements that test data not predate training data and splits respect dependency structure [[220707048-leakage-and-the-reproducibility-crisis-in-ml-based-science]][[overview-of-leakage-scenarios-in-supervised-machine-learning-journal-of-big-data]]. Repeated-subject leakage inflates performance [[data-leakage-inflates-prediction-performance-in-connectome-based-machine-learnin]], and the small-set amplifier for same-thread tweets in a 150 to 250 golden set needs verification against full text. Out-of-distribution degradation is the norm [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

Rebuild before any other measurement:

- Slice one brand preserving tweet and author and inbound and timestamp and text and both linkage columns.
- Define threads as connected components with start time and position and size; quarantine orphans from golden eligibility.
- Collapse near-duplicates by normalized-text exact plus SimHash/embedding threshold keeping one golden-eligible representative.
- Sort threads by start time, place knowledge base and train and few-shot pools strictly before cutoff T1 with golden strictly after, assigning straddlers by start time. Partition everything with GroupShuffleSplit semantics on thread identifier (fractions refer to groups not samples) [[groupshufflesplit-scikit-learn-191-documentation]] plus a fail-closed zero-overlap assertion on every harness run, with TimeSeriesSplit-style gap mapping to the no-straddle-by-start-time rule [[timeseriessplit-scikit-learn-191-documentation]] and author overlap reported separately. Restrict the knowledge base to pre-cutoff outbound, exclude same-thread documents at eval time, and report scores with and without exclusion as the leakage diagnostic. Stratify threads, not tweets, by weak-intent bucket and escalate flag and size bucket, backfilling rare strata only from latest pre-cutoff threads with per-stratum counts disclosed in the golden note.

**Stamp every golden row** with tweet and thread and timestamp and stratum. Random tweet sampling is acceptable only for explicitly scoped interpolation claims with both disjointness and exclusion holding — nearly worthless for the trust argument graded here. The rebuild uses Polars with DuckDB over Parquet on tutorial-scale speedups without distributed machinery [[python-data-pipelines-with-polars-and-duckdb-andrew-odendaal]][[modern-dataframes-in-python-a-hands-on-tutorial-with-polars-and-duckdb-towards-d]], NOT MEASURED on this slice; the report times its own ETL wall-clock rather than quoting tutorials as results. Until the rebuild lands, every workspace number is an upper bound.

## 3. Intent Taxonomy — Deriving a Small Routable Measurable Label Scheme

Two independent squeezes force 6 to 8 intents plus out-of-scope. Controlled experiments show LLM out-of-scope detection degrades faster than encoder detection as scope and count grow, favoring small fine-grained spaces [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. A 200-item golden set with a 20-per-intent minimum caps the scheme at 10 absolute, with decision and difficulty and temporal strata pushing the practical ceiling near 7. The size is over-determined, not stylistic.

Banking77 is the counter-model. Its 13,083 queries across 77 intents are clean banking English [[polyaibanking77-datasets-at-hugging-face]], with the 3,080-test split per [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]] (10,003 train disclosed as 13,083-minus-3,080 arithmetic). No handles, no threads, no sarcasm, no truncation spikes. Seventy-seven overlapping classes maximize confusion and prompt failure and per-label starvation at golden scale. Use Banking77 for harness prototyping, router tuning, and augmentation calibration only — never mixed into brand pools and never quoted beside brand accuracy.

Derive from brand threads in an afternoon. Embed a thread-aware sample with a Twitter-pretrained encoder. Reduce and cluster without forcing every ticket into a cluster, then describe clusters with class-based TF-IDF [[bertopic-bertopic-latest-documentation]]. The BERT plus HDBSCAN plus class-TF-IDF pattern is the established ticket-theme recipe [[ml-case-study-interview-question-discovering-support-ticket-themes-with-bert-emb]], with the 5,000-plus-conversation / 0.63-coherence demonstration per [[github-kelukesvps-support-ticket-clustering-clustering-and-topic-modeling-of-vps]] (numbers absent from the case-study note). Inspect 15 to 20 threads per cluster. Merge clusters sharing a resolution action; split clusters whose resolutions diverge. Resolution action, not vocabulary, is the merge key. Write one-line scope definitions plus boundary examples, pilot 30 items, revise from disagreements, then label gold. Enforce a 15 percent cross-confusion merge rule: annotator-indistinguishable pairs cannot support routing or escalation. Mask label names to neutral identifiers during LLM prompting per the controlled protocol [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]].

The expected AmazonHelp shape — order tracking, refund and charge dispute, damaged or wrong item, shipping and carrier problem, account access failure, setup how-to, complaint escalation, plus out-of-scope — is NOT MEASURED until clustering runs. Each intent needs a playbook pointer: resolving outbound patterns, required slots before auto-handling, and always-escalate variants. Intents without distinct resolutions are topic labels, not routing targets.

Out-of-scope needs emphasis because all families struggle. Encoder recall near 0.38 and best-LLM recall near 0.60 to 0.64 at best-F1 — recall figures, not F1 — are the controlled reference (SetFit 0.378, Haiku 0.601, Mistral-L 0.641). Negative augmentation lifts the encoder substantially [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]] and belongs in the recipe (exact one-fifth-of-training-sentences ratio unverified in the fetched body). GPT synthesis is capped at 7 per intent, because:

> GPT-generated data significantly boosts the performance of intent classifiers when intents in consideration are sufficiently distinct from each other

[[data-augmentation-for-intent-classification-with-off-the-shelf-large-language-mo]], while close pairs like delayed versus lost versus billing versus refund sit in the bleed regime where generation produces neighbor-intent utterances. Keep synthesis as a tiny ESTIMATED ablation, never the rare-intent fix.

## 4. System Architecture — Minimal Design That Maximizes Trust per Complexity

No agent framework ships, and the rejection is load-bearing. Orchestration with hundreds of integrations earns overhead for multi-step tool loops, but plain Python with direct calls is faster to build and debug and maintain for single-pass generation [[is-langchain-becoming-too-complexbloated-for-simple-rag-applications-in-2025-com]]. This pipeline is single-pass: classify, retrieve thread-window context, draft once, gate once, with one pre-registered re-attempt before escalation. No planning loop or tool choice justifies a graph orchestrator. If the pipeline fits in direct calls, it does not need the framework [[rag-vs-langchain-which-do-you-need]]. The declarative self-improving alternative has real adoption [[github-stanfordnlpdspy-dspy-the-framework-for-programmingnot-promptinglanguage-m]]; fairly, DSPy-style programming-not-prompting is the natural challenger for optimizing the two most fragile prompts here (constrained generator, caged judge) behind the same groundedness and agreement gates, with revival if it moves groundedness or judge agreement on brand gold. No graph orchestrator ships now, but the one pre-registered retrieve-grade re-attempt already crosses the stated revisit threshold, so any second loop forces the framework question open.

Four stages share one confidence doctrine:

- **Classify** — SetFit-first intent with negative augmentation [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]], on a Twitter backbone [[bertweet-a-pre-trained-language-model-for-english-tweets-acl-anthology]], routes uncertain items to a low-cost model with retrieval-filtered few-shot examples.
- **Retrieve** — hybrid BM25 plus dense retrieval over thread windows with cold-start fusion and later tuned weighting plus rerank of at most 50 supplies top contexts [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]].
- **Draft** — constrained generation drafts only from retrieved history with an entailment veto.
- **Gate** — temperature-scaled MaxProb plus entailment governs escalation, with a learned calibrator admitted only on cross-split proof [[200809371-towards-improving-selective-prediction-ability-of-nlp-systems]][[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

Order is the integration argument. Splits produce strata, strata supply identifier versus paraphrase slices for retrieval ablation, ablation deltas feed the grounding gate, and agreement gates certify the judge downstream. Reversing the order reproduces the audited circularity where keyword labels mirror the simple baseline.

Classifier numbers need scope. On six dialogue datasets SetFit averages 0.600 F1 at 0.030 seconds, negative augmentation lifts it past 0.65, and the best LLM averages 0.736 at 1.7 seconds [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. The routed hybrid lands within about 2 points of the LLM outside shift but about 4 points inside shift (M-equals-10-plus-Haiku 0.737 ex-OADP vs 0.696 incl-OADP against native 0.760/0.736), at roughly half the latency while routing 0.32 to 0.43 of queries to the LLM. ADP-to-OADP SetFit drops about 15 percent versus a smaller LLM drop, and OOS recall runs Mistral-L 0.641, Haiku 0.601, SNA 0.489, baseline SetFit 0.378. Conditional SetFit-first is therefore shift-conditional: it reverses to LLM-first if the incl-shift gap replicates on the brand temporal split:

> with the hybrid system we are able to bring performance gap further down to ~2% (from ~6%) for all datasets for which train and test data were from same distribution [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. Per-query speed is reported as:

> SetFit is about 56 times faster than overall best LLM (v3 Haiku)

[[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. Retrieval-filtered top-20 few-shot reaches 85.5 percent at 42 dollars versus 83.1 percent at 740 dollars for a 231-example full prompt on the same test set — cost/accuracy numbers per [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]], and cascades match best singles at large savings or improve at equal cost — cascade claim per [[230505176-frugalgpt-how-to-use-large-language-models-while-reducing-cost-and-imp]]. These are clean-dialogue figures, not Twitter proof. Close the gap with a Twitter-pretrained backbone, which beats generic baselines on tweet tagging and entity and classification [[bertweet-a-pre-trained-language-model-for-english-tweets-acl-anthology]], with the IntentBERT pre-training effect supporting few-shot intent transfer [[effectiveness-of-pre-training-for-few-shot-intent-classification-acl-anthology]] (generic-versus-Twitter backbone comparison NOT MEASURED on brand gold; backbone choice to be confirmed by ablation). Start from the prompt-free contrastive SetFit recipe [[220911055-efficient-few-shot-learning-without-prompts]][[github-huggingfacesetfit-efficient-few-shot-learning-with-sentence-transformers]].

Routing is a contest, not a default. Dropout sampling approximates Bayesian uncertainty but underestimates it, and raw softmax is not confidence [[dropout-as-a-bayesian-approximation-representing-model-uncertainty-in-deep-learn]]. The 17-dataset comparison warns:

> none of the existing approaches consistently and considerably outperforms MaxProb in all three settings [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]]. Full-layer 10-pass dropout (dropout 0.1, M equals 10; M equals 5 captures most gain, 10-to-20 flat, batched MC cuts latency) routes under the Arora App A.2 predicate — uncertain iff stochastic forwards show more than one distinct argmax or fewer than M/2 agree — and only if it beats temperature-scaled MaxProb picked by AURC/refinement on IID-held-out and temporal-held-out per the MaxProb gate [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

LLM few-shot with filtered prompts is the fallback the contest can promote, since in-context models beat fine-tuned non-generative ones on few examples and wording variance [[230814634-breaking-the-bank-with-chatgpt-few-shot-text-classification-for-financ]]. If the router fails on AUGRC across both splits with a Twitter backbone, the recommendation reverses to LLM-first. Embedded stores answer top-10 under 30 ms at this scale with rerank dominating end-to-end [[chromadb-vs-qdrant-vs-faiss-vs-pgvector-vector-database-for-local-rag-local-llmn]], so in-process BM25 plus a local dense index keeps the 15 minute repro free of containers. ## 5. Retrieval and Reply Generation — Grounding Replies in Historical Resolutions

Unretrieved truth cannot be generated. Dense-only is the worst default here for a structural reason. Pooling token vectors into one fixed vector buys paraphrase tolerance but destroys rare-string identity, while an inverted index either finds the term or not, with rarity helping via inverse document frequency [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. Support tweets concentrate dense-hostile features — handles, tags, order numbers, versions, URLs, typos, 2 to 5 word queries — in under 280 characters. Zero-shot dense trained on web passages often fails to beat BM25 out-of-distribution [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]].

The failure is silent and trust-critical:

> Pure dense retrieval fails silently on exact identifiers, code, and rare terms [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]]. A miss still returns something, the model writes a fluent confident reply with wrong specifics, no error fires, and production-derived eval sets exclude these cases. Blended recall can look acceptable while identifier recall collapses toward 40 percent — ESTIMATED from diagnosis literature, NOT MEASURED on this brand until ablation.

Index thread windows with 50 to 100 tokens of prepended context — an author instantiation (token budget and brand/thread/turn/issue/prior-turn fields) built on the prepended-context concept [[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]].

Contextual BM25 cuts failures by about 49 percent, plus rerank about 67 percent cross-domain [[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]] (no 35 percent top-20 figure in the fetched body; deleted pending re-sourcing). Preserve handles and digits and hyphens and versions in the BM25 tokenizer with light stemming and short-document normalization; use a short-text encoder for dense. Fuse 100 candidates per arm with cold-start RRF score(d) equals sum 1/(k plus rank), k equals 60, sidestepping BM25-versus-cosine scale, then migrate to weighted score equals alpha times dense_norm plus (1 minus alpha) times sparse_norm only after 40-plus relevance pairs via alpha-sweep 0 to 1. Domain priors start alpha near 0.3 sparse-heavy for identifier traffic, 0.7 to 0.8 conversational, 0.5 to 0.6 balanced; normalization is second-order but depth-coupled so depths are logged with alpha. BM25 k1 0.5 to 0.8 for short docs with preserved @/#/digits/hyphens; WANDS shows naive fusion 0.7068 versus 0.6983/0.6953 (plus about 1.2 percent) versus tuned-plus-boost 0.7497 (plus 7.4 percent), tiered plus 7.5 versus plain plus 1.3, justifying the 5-point identifier gate [[hybrid-search-for-rag-combining-bm25-and-dense-vector-search-2026-guide]]. Deduplicate with maximal-marginal-relevance, rerank at most 30 to 50 with a cross-encoder, pass top 5 to 10 with per-stage ranks logged.

External deltas set expectations, not brand proof. Tech-docs stacks move 0.67 dense-only to 0.78 hybrid to 0.84 with rerank. Tuned weighting with field boosts adds 7-plus points where naive fusion adds about 1 [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]]. Exact-term lift of 8 to 15 percent concentrates on exact-term queries [[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. WANDS naive-versus-tuned and financial recall-at-5 tables are specified in the six-arm ablation below [[hybrid-search-for-rag-combining-bm25-and-dense-vector-search-2026-guide]]. Direction is committed on structural grounds. Identifier traffic keeps a lexical arm because pooled dense vectors destroy rare-string identity while an inverted index preserves it [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. Only magnitude and form are gated: cold-start fusion versus tuned alpha versus document-only SPLADE single-index ships on 5-point identifier-stratum recall-at-10 gain without blended regression and within latency/ops budget, else the tuned form demotes to documented over-engineering while the lexical arm stays. Rerank discipline is strict: 30 to 50 candidates cost 100 to 200 ms, and needing 200-plus signals broken first-stage recall [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]]. Two indices plus rerank carry real ops cost — versioning, timeout budgets, fusion edge cases, attribution logging — borne only if the gate clears.
