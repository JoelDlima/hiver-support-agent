# Hiver Support Agent: Forensic Audit and Trustworthy Re-engineering Plan for One Brand on Customer Support on Twitter

Existing workspace headline numbers are uninterpolated upper bounds until a thread-aware temporal rebuild with retrieval exclusion is completed. On that rebuilt foundation, trust per complexity is maximized by a plain dependency-ordered pipeline — conditional SetFit-first routing, gated hybrid retrieval, caged pointwise judging with tiered gates, and a scaled MaxProb escalation floor scored by AUGRC — where every complexity upgrade ships only behind a pre-registered brand-data measurement with explicit reversal conditions.

The assignment picks one brand from Customer Support on Twitter, builds intent classification plus grounded reply generation plus an auto-handle versus escalate decider, and proves trustworthiness with a 150 to 250 item golden set, an evaluation harness, LLM-as-judge plus human agreement, two baselines, a concise report, and a decision log [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. The proof is the product. The system described here is deliberately minimal because every added stage must earn its place on rebuilt brand data within a 15 minute reproduction budget.

## 1. Problem Framing — What Good and Trustworthy Mean for One Brand

Good for one brand means three verifiable behaviors on future customer messages. The intent label routes to the correct handling path. The drafted reply is entailed by how that brand actually resolved similar threads. The auto-handle versus escalate call matches what a careful human handler would do, with a stated reason every time [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Trustworthy adds the proof layer. Those three behaviors are measured on a conversation-disjoint temporally posterior golden set of 150 to 250 hand-labelled threads, scored against two honest baselines, with an LLM judge whose agreement with humans is itself measured and gated before any headline is quoted [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]][[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]].

This framing is narrow by design. One brand means one issue distribution, one voice, one resolution playbook. Trust evidence at n equals 200 has statistical meaning only inside that scope [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. A system that looks adequate averaged across a retailer, a device maker, and an airline can be unsafe on each individually, and per-label agreement math at n equals 200 already forces a small taxonomy with no headroom to cover cross-brand variation [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]].

What good explicitly excludes matters as much as what it includes. The agent classifies, drafts, and defers. It does not execute actions, issue refunds, change accounts, or call tools. Any sensitive action, missing slot needed for safe action, or low-confidence grounding escalates with a reason code rather than generating an answer [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. The handoff shows the reason and the evidence while suppressing the uncertain draft unless a handler explicitly requests it, because informing handlers of deferral without surfacing the uncertain guess improves downstream human accuracy while revealing the low-confidence guess degrades it [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]][[selective-prediction-tasks]].

Four contracts make the claim testable. Intent classification is good when 6 to 8 mutually distinguishable intents route correctly on future threads, with out-of-scope detection that degrades gracefully rather than forcing a wrong label [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. Reply generation is good when every actionable claim is entailed by cited retrieved brand history, the next step matches brand playbook for channel and link and ask, and no policy, compensation, timeline, or personal data is invented [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Escalation is good when the auto-handled slice meets a pre-registered risk target and every escalation carries a coded reason plus evidence, not only a score [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Reproducibility is good when a stranger reproduces the headline in under 15 minutes from a frozen subsample without special hardware [[if-you-build-your-own-ner-scorer-non-replicable-results-will-come-acl-anthology]].

Deliberately not built: multi-brand generality, live channel integration, multi-step agent tool loops, learned sparse retrieval, dynamic per-query fusion weights, fine-tuned generation, fine-tuned judging, streaming evaluation, production secrets management beyond redaction, and any claim about full 3M-row scale. Each exclusion is a bet that the complexity buys less trust than the same hours spent on golden-set adjudication and leakage control. Each has a documented revival condition later in this report. Deployment on new threads over time is an out-of-distribution problem by definition [[211011334-generalized-out-of-distribution-detection-a-survey]], and standard metrics computed on interpolating splits do not license deployment claims [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

## 2. Data Foundation — Conversation Graph, Brand Selection, Splits, and Leakage Control

The raw Customer Support on Twitter data is not a table of independent tweets. Each row carries tweet identifier, anonymized author identifier, inbound flag, creation timestamp, text with masked private data, plus reply linkage fields that reconstruct entire threads [[customer-support-on-twitter-openbigdataorg]]. The corpus is widely mirrored as the approximately 3M-row thoughtvector source [[github-amruta33customer-support-on-twitter-the-customer-support-on-twitter-datas]]. Any step that flattens threads to isolated pairs silently removes the grouping key the evaluation must respect.

Two derivatives illustrate the choice. The TNE-AI conversation build preserves threading explicitly across 794,000 rows and 109 companies [[tne-aicustomer-support-on-twitter-conversation-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. The 945,000-row input-output derivative does the opposite by distilling multi-turn threads into normalized input-output pairs with slang and emoji and URL and mention cleaning [[mohammadothmanmo-customer-support-tweets-945k-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. The latter is convenient for fine-tuning and unusable for leakage auditing because the thread identifier is gone. The implementable rule is direct. Never adopt a pair-flattened derivative as the primary source. Rebuild from raw twcs data and carry identifiers forward on every artifact.

Brand selection should maximize submission quality, not row count. Per-brand exploratory analysis on the exact corpus reports AmazonHelp at 169,840 tweets and 168,823 responses with median response 11.47 minutes, AppleSupport at 106,860 tweets and 106,648 responses with median 70.97 minutes, TMobileHelp at 34,317 tweets and 34,229 responses with median 2.75 minutes, AmericanAir at 36,764 tweets with median 10.73 minutes, and British Airways at 29,361 tweets with median 180.5 minutes [[github-abh2050customer_support_intelligence-an-interactive-streamlit-app-leverag]]. High-volume brands with structured playbooks support grounding while slow responders with sparse resolutions undermine it [[github-abh2050customer_support_intelligence-an-interactive-streamlit-app-leverag]]. Textual-analysis precedents show the corpus supports count-vectorizer and TF-IDF and Word2Vec and GloVe and FastText exploration with context and product and location perspectives [[github-jessica1438twitter-customer-support-dataset-textual-analysis-and-perspect]].

Recommendation: build on AmazonHelp with AmericanAir as documented fallback. AmazonHelp gives the largest pre-cutoff knowledge base, the most stable post-cutoff golden window, and an issue space around order status and shipping and refund and damaged items and account access that compresses naturally to 6 to 8 distinguishable intents. If AmazonHelp threads prove too short for thread-window retrieval study, AmericanAir is the fallback because its flight-number identifiers stress the hybrid-retrieval hypothesis most cleanly. TMobileHelp remains a one-line alternate where fast templated resolutions make auto-handle defensible. The report reproduces this table with rejected brands named.

The forensic audit of the current workspace is severe and specific. The headline Apple inbound pool holds 97,592 rows with columns limited to tweet and author and inbound and text variants, with no thread identifier and no timestamp, so conversation-disjoint or temporal splitting is impossible from that artifact [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. The golden file holds 200 rows with text plus labels and no tweet or thread or time keys, so provenance is untraceable and overlap with training and few-shot and retrieval pools is unauditable by construction [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. All 200 golden texts match pool texts verbatim, confirming the golden set was drawn from the same population that feeds training and retrieval [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. The knowledge base holds 89,694 rows that are outbound with tweet identifiers preserved, which structurally means it contains the brand gold responses while golden queries are inbound from the same brand threads, but same-thread target-response containment cannot be checked because neither artifact carries thread identifiers, so the highest-value contamination of the retriever fetching the exact gold answer with the judge rewarding near-perfect groundedness is currently unmeasurable, which is itself the finding [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. A thread-preserving artifact with thread and timestamp and position and gap-split columns exists elsewhere in the repository but was not applied to the headline pipeline [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

Three leakage mechanisms are therefore live, not hypothetical. Same-thread overlap means consecutive turns share entities and phrasing and resolution state, so a classifier or retriever that saw one turn effectively knows the next [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Retrieval of the target response means the knowledge base built from brand outbound tweets contains the gold answer to many evaluation queries [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Future-answering-past means policy and outage status and links drift across the collection window, so training on later tweets to answer earlier ones is temporal leakage as formally defined. Test data should not predate training data and splits should respect dependency structure through block partitioning on threads [[220707048-leakage-and-the-reproducibility-crisis-in-ml-based-science]][[overview-of-leakage-scenarios-in-supervised-machine-learning-journal-of-big-data]]. Measured work on repeated-subject leakage shows performance inflation that worsens on small evaluation sets, exactly the combination present here where same-thread tweets act as repeated subjects and the golden set holds only 150 to 250 items [[data-leakage-inflates-prediction-performance-in-connectome-based-machine-learnin]]. Deployment on new threads is out-of-distribution, and in-distribution-only numbers do not license deployment claims [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

The rebuild recipe is mechanical and must precede every other measurement.

1. Rebuild with linkage. Slice one brand preserving tweet and author and inbound and timestamp and text and both reply-linkage columns. Never drop these columns downstream. Pool and knowledge base and golden all carry tweet and thread and timestamp.
2. Reconstruct threads. Connected components over the reply graph define thread identifier. Thread start is minimum timestamp. Record position in thread and thread size. Quarantine orphans with no linkage into a separate stratum excluded from golden eligibility.
3. Collapse near-duplicates. Normalized-text exact match plus SimHash or embedding threshold flags template replies and reposts across threads. Keep one representative golden-eligible and mark the rest train or knowledge-base only.
4. Impose a temporal cutoff. Sort threads by start time. Knowledge base plus classifier train plus few-shot pool end at or before T1. Golden and eval window starts strictly after T1. No thread straddles T1. Assign by start time. The headline becomes a forward-looking claim.
5. Enforce conversation-disjointness everywhere. Every partition uses group-aware splitting on thread identifier with zero-overlap assertion between golden and any train or knowledge-base or few-shot artifact, evaluated as a fail-closed harness check on every run.
6. Exclude the retrieval answer. Knowledge base is pre-cutoff outbound only. At evaluation time exclude any knowledge-base document from the query own thread as defense in depth against linkage errors. Report scores with and without the exclusion as the leakage diagnostic.
7. Stratify threads, not tweets. Within the post-cutoff window stratify threads by weak-intent bucket and escalate flag and thread-size bucket. If a rare intent is unfillable post-cutoff, backfill from the latest pre-cutoff threads for that stratum only and disclose per-stratum counts.
8. Publish provenance. Golden columns are tweet and thread and timestamp and text and intent and escalate and reason and stratum. Re-run overlap assertions on every harness invocation.

Random tweet-level stratified sampling is acceptable only when the claim is explicitly scoped to static-snapshot interpolation with thread-disjointness plus retrieval exclusion both holding. That scoped claim is nearly worthless for the trust argument the assignment grades, and the current workspace meets neither condition [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. What would overturn the rebuild requirement is a controlled ablation on rebuilt threaded data showing under a 2-point delta on both intent accuracy and judge-groundedness with exclusion on versus off, with intervals from the agreement-gated judge. Until then every workspace number is labeled an upper bound. Data engineering for the rebuild uses Polars with DuckDB over Parquet rather than pandas over CSV, on the standard tutorial speedups at multi-million-row scale without distributed machinery [[python-data-pipelines-with-polars-and-duckdb-andrew-odendaal]][[modern-dataframes-in-python-a-hands-on-tutorial-with-polars-and-duckdb-towards-d]]. That speedup is NOT MEASURED on this exact brand slice. The report times its own ETL wall-clock on the reference machine rather than quoting tutorial numbers as results.

## 3. Intent Taxonomy — Deriving a Small Routable Measurable Label Scheme

The taxonomy is squeezed from both ends, which is why 6 to 8 intents plus an explicit out-of-scope class is the committed size. From the model side, controlled experiments show LLM out-of-scope detection degrades faster than encoder performance as label scope broadens and label count grows, with the direct guidance that fine-grained labels and smaller label spaces favor LLM out-of-scope handling [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. From the annotation side, a 200-item golden set with a 20-per-intent minimum imposes an absolute ceiling of 10 intents, and once decision and difficulty and temporal strata with their own minimums are added, the practical ceiling falls to about 7 [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. When model behavior and annotation arithmetic independently point to the same narrow band, the size is over-determined rather than stylistic.

Banking77 with 77 intents is the counter-model, not the template. It comprises 13,083 banking queries across 77 intents with 10,003 training and 3,080 test examples, clean single-domain banking English [[polyaibanking77-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. It exists to test fine-grained single-domain intent detection. It is explicitly not Twitter. No handles, no thread context, no sarcasm, no truncation, no outage spikes. Seventy-seven overlapping classes maximize confusion and prompt-length failure and per-label data starvation at golden-set scale. Banking77 is therefore legitimately used for intent-method prototyping only — calibrating the classifier harness, tuning the router, validating the augmentation sweet spot — and never to inflate headline numbers, which must come solely from the brand own threaded golden set.

Derivation must be data-driven and documented. The defensible sequence runs in an afternoon. Embed a thread-aware brand sample with a Twitter-pretrained encoder. Reduce and cluster without forcing every ticket into a cluster, and describe clusters with class-based TF-IDF [[bertopic-bertopic-latest-documentation]]. The BERT plus HDBSCAN plus class-TF-IDF pattern is the established ticket-theme recipe [[ml-case-study-interview-question-discovering-support-ticket-themes-with-bert-emb]], demonstrated on 5,000-plus multilingual support conversations with coherence 0.63 and manual review [[github-kelukesvps-support-ticket-clustering-clustering-and-topic-modeling-of-vps]]. Inspect top terms plus 15 to 20 representative threads per candidate cluster against actual threads, not isolated tweets. Merge clusters that share a resolution action even if wording differs. Split clusters whose resolutions diverge even if wording overlaps. Resolution action, not vocabulary, is the merge key. Write one-line scope definitions plus 2 to 3 boundary examples per intent before labeling anything. Pilot 30 items, revise the guideline from disagreements, then label the golden set. Enforce distinguishability mechanically. Any pair exceeding roughly 15 percent cross-confusion merges before headlines are quoted. If two intents cannot be told apart by trained annotators at pilot scale, no classifier routing on them can be trusted and no escalation policy conditioned on them is meaningful. Mask label names to neutral identifiers during LLM prompting to prevent spurious name memorization, following the controlled protocol behind the load-bearing hybrid numbers [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]].

A brand agent of this shape typically converges on order status tracking, refund and return and charge dispute, damaged or wrong item, shipping and carrier problem, account access and authentication failure, product setup how-to, complaint escalation and callback request, plus explicit out-of-scope-other. This shape is NOT MEASURED for this brand until the rebuild clustering pass is executed and is listed here as the expected shape, not a finding. Each intent needs a resolution-playbook pointer: which historical outbound patterns resolve it, which slots are required before safe auto-handling, and which variants always escalate. Intents without distinct resolutions are topic labels, and topic labels do not route.

The out-of-scope class deserves special emphasis because every model struggles with it. Out-of-scope recall at best-F1 is poor across both LLM and encoder families in controlled tables, with encoder baselines near 0.38 average and the best LLMs near 0.60 to 0.64 [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. Negative augmentation — removing or random-string-replacing keywords in a fifth of training sentences and treating the results as out-of-scope — lifts the encoder substantially and replicates across datasets [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. That augmentation is therefore part of the committed classifier recipe, while GPT synthetic generation is capped at a tiny ablation of at most 7 synthetic items per intent, because beyond that point real-data comparisons show degradation and the bleed mechanism is worst on exactly the close-intent pairs a support taxonomy contains [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]][[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. Controlled work states the constraint directly:

> GPT-generated data significantly boosts the performance of intent classifiers when intents in consideration are sufficiently distinct from each other

[[data-augmentation-for-intent-classification-with-off-the-shelf-large-language-mo]]. A brand taxonomy with delayed versus lost versus damaged, or billing versus refund, sits in the failure regime where synthetic data cross-contaminates by generating neighbor-intent utterances. Permit at most a tiny synthetic ablation labeled ESTIMATED until golden-gated, never as the primary fix for rare-intent starvation.

## 4. System Architecture — Minimal Design That Maximizes Trust per Complexity

The committed architecture is a plain pipeline with no agent framework, and the framework rejection is load-bearing rather than aesthetic. Current-generation orchestration ships stable with hundreds of integrations and earns its overhead for multi-step agent workflows with tool loops, but practitioner consensus across independent sources is that plain Python with direct API calls is faster to build and debug and maintain for single-pass retrieval-augmented generation [[is-langchain-becoming-too-complexbloated-for-simple-rag-applications-in-2025-com]]. This submission is single-pass by design: classify, retrieve thread-window context, draft once, gate once, with a single pre-registered re-attempt before final escalation. There is no planning loop, no tool choice, no reflection cycle that would justify a graph orchestrator. The decision rule experienced developers use is dispositive. If the pipeline fits in under roughly 100 lines with direct calls, it does not need the framework [[rag-vs-langchain-which-do-you-need]]. This one does. Log the graph-framework path as a considered rejection with the reversal condition of adopting it if and only if the escalation policy grows a genuine loop with re-retrieval and grading and fallback, rather than as ignorance of its existence. The programmatic alternative that compiles declarative calls into self-improving pipelines has substantial adoption [[github-stanfordnlpdspy-dspy-the-framework-for-programmingnot-promptinglanguage-m]], but it answers a different problem than this take-home poses.

The pipeline has four stages with one shared confidence doctrine. First, a SetFit-first intent classifier on a Twitter-pretrained backbone with cheap negative augmentation routes only uncertain items to a low-cost instruction model with retrieval-filtered dynamic few-shot examples [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]][[bertweet-a-pre-trained-language-model-for-english-tweets-acl-anthology]]. Second, a hybrid BM25 plus dense retriever over thread-window chunks with reciprocal-rank fusion cold-start and cross-validated weighting once labels exist and cross-encoder rerank of at most 50 candidates supplies the top 5 to 10 grounded contexts [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]]. Third, a reply generator drafts strictly from retrieved brand history with an entailment gate that can veto auto-handling independently of intent confidence [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Fourth, an escalation policy built on temperature-scaled maximum softmax probability plus that entailment gate, with a learned calibrator admitted only on proof of cross-split wins, makes the final auto-handle call with a five-code reason on every escalation [[200809371-towards-improving-selective-prediction-ability-of-nlp-systems]][[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

The dependency order is the systems-integration argument. Splits produce strata, strata supply the identifier versus paraphrase slices the retrieval ablation needs, the ablation groundedness delta feeds the escalation grounding gate, and the golden-set agreement gates certify the judge that scores everything downstream. Presenting it any other way — judge first, splits later — invites the exact circularity the audit found where keyword labels are circular with the simple baseline [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

The classifier choice is the most argued stage, so the numbers need stating precisely with their scope. On six real task-oriented-dialogue datasets, the SetFit baseline averages 0.600 F1 at 0.030 seconds per query, negative augmentation lifts it above 0.65, the best native LLM averages 0.736 at 1.7 seconds, and the uncertainty-routed hybrid lands within about 2 points of the native LLM outside distribution shift at roughly half the latency while routing only a third to under half of queries to the LLM [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. The hybrid paper states the gap closure directly:

> with the hybrid system we are able to bring performance gap further down to ~2% (from ~6%) for all datasets for which train and test data were from same distribution

[[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. SetFit runs an order of magnitude faster per query, reported as:

> SetFit is about 56 times faster than overall best LLM (v3 Haiku)

[[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. The cost companion is equally stark. Retrieval-filtered dynamic few-shot with the top 20 neighbors reaches 85.5 percent at 42 dollars where a full 231-example classic prompt reaches 83.1 percent at 740 dollars on the same 3,080-query test set — cheaper and better simultaneously because the classic approach hits context and cost walls that dynamic filtering sidesteps [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]]. Cost-cascade work shows learned routing across models matching the best single model at large cost reduction or improving accuracy at equal cost [[230505176-frugalgpt-how-to-use-large-language-models-while-reducing-cost-and-imp]]. Those figures come from clean banking and shopping dialogue rather than noisy tweets, so they must not be quoted as Twitter proof. The transfer gap is closed by swapping the generic paraphrase backbone for a Twitter-pretrained encoder, since the first large-scale tweet model with the standard base architecture trained under the robust pretraining procedure beats generic strong baselines on tweet tagging and entity and classification tasks [[bertweet-a-pre-trained-language-model-for-english-tweets-acl-anthology]]. Noisy-tweet benchmarks favor Twitter-pretrained backbones, and a single SetFit run on a generic backbone did not beat them and trained slower [[fine-tuning-language-models-on-dutch-protest-event-tweets-acl-anthology]]. So the backbone is Twitter-pretrained, with a generic MPNet ablation quantifying the transfer gap rather than assuming it. The SetFit reference implementation without prompts plus a classification head is the harness starting point [[220911055-efficient-few-shot-learning-without-prompts]][[github-huggingfacesetfit-efficient-few-shot-learning-with-sentence-transformers]].

Routing mechanics carry a mandatory contest. Monte Carlo dropout as a Bayesian approximation samples multiple stochastic forward passes and treats prediction variance as uncertainty, but its variational character typically underestimates uncertainty and raw softmax outputs must never be read as confidence [[dropout-as-a-bayesian-approximation-representing-model-uncertainty-in-deep-learn]]. The large-scale selective-prediction study across 17 datasets finds a sobering baseline:

> none of the existing approaches consistently and considerably outperforms MaxProb in all three settings

[[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]]. Dropout wins duplicate detection but fails natural-language inference especially out-of-domain in that study. The architecture therefore treats full-layer Monte Carlo dropout with 10 passes as the default router while requiring it to beat temperature-scaled MaxProb on area under the risk-coverage curve on both held-out and temporal slices before it governs either routing or escalation. A narrower study shows full-layer dropout can beat MaxProb substantially on in-domain text-classification rejection curves [[uncertainty-estimation-of-transformer-predictions-for-misclassification-detectio]], which is why dropout stays the challenger rather than being discarded. Single-pass softmax remains the cost default until the challenger proves ranking wins, consistent with efficient single-pass approximations competing with multi-pass estimates at far lower cost [[221014037-revisiting-softmax-for-uncertainty-approximation-in-text-classificatio]]. Native-LLM few-shot with retrieval-filtered prompts is the fallback the contest can promote. In-context learning with large models beats fine-tuned non-generative models with fewer examples and handles wording variance that dominates Twitter [[230814634-breaking-the-bank-with-chatgpt-few-shot-text-classification-for-financ]]. If the SetFit router cannot beat the scaled MaxProb floor on AUGRC across both splits with a Twitter backbone, the recommendation reverses to LLM-first with retrieval-filtered prompts. That reversal condition is pre-registered, not improvised.

## 5. Retrieval and Reply Generation — Grounding Replies in Historical Resolutions

Retrieval is the grounding bottleneck. Whatever the retriever fails to return, the generator cannot truthfully say. Pure dense retrieval is the worst default on support tweets for a structural reason agreed across every retrieval source. A bi-encoder pools all token representations into one fixed vector, which buys paraphrase generalization but destroys the lexical identity of rare strings. An error code becomes a vector about errors in general rather than the document containing that exact string, while an inverted index either finds the term or does not, with rarity helping rather than hurting [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. Twitter support concentrates every dense-hostile feature inside a few hundred characters: handles, hashtags, order and tracking numbers, version strings, URLs, typos, abbreviations, and 2 to 5 word queries with almost no semantic content [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Cross-domain zero-shot precedent shows dense models trained on web passages frequently failing to beat BM25 outside their training distribution, which is why serious teams treat dense as a complement rather than a replacement [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]].

The failure is silent, which is what makes it trust-critical. > Pure dense retrieval fails silently on exact identifiers, code, and rare terms

## 6. Escalation Policy — Automating Only What Is Safe to Automate

Escalation is selective prediction with a coverage price and a graded reason attached. Errors here directly harm customers. A false auto-handle sends a wrong answer with the brand voice, while a false escalation merely costs handler time. The policy must therefore be risk-targeted and reason-coded and shift-aware, with coverage reported alongside every risk number [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

The confidence doctrine is unified across routing and escalation. Temperature-scaled MaxProb is the robustness floor. Challengers are admitted only on cross-split wins. Thresholds lock on a calibration slice. Golden is touched once. The primary signal is temperature-scaled maximum softmax probability of the intent head. Modern networks are systematically overconfident and single-parameter temperature scaling fit on held-out likelihood is the most effective post-hoc fix on most vision and document sets [[supplementary-materials-foron-calibration-of-modern-neural-networks]]. Temperature scaling cuts expected calibration error substantially without accuracy loss in controlled text studies [[reliability-aware-bert-emotion-classification-2026-note-placeholder-FIXME]]. The mandatory conjunction is the entailment gate above. Intent confidence never licenses auto-handling of an ungrounded draft. A learned confidence-plus-difficulty calibrator of the published form reports double-digit risk-coverage gains over MaxProb on inference and duplicate-detection tasks [[200809371-towards-improving-selective-prediction-ability-of-nlp-systems]], but it is admitted only if it beats scaled MaxProb on both area under the risk-coverage curve and a ranking-quality refinement metric on held-out and temporal slices alike, because the larger follow-up study shows those gains do not transfer reliably across tasks and settings [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]]. Monotone scaling improves calibration error and threshold interpretability but cannot repair ranking, so ordering quality must be measured separately before any learned signal is trusted [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Refinement isolates confidence-function quality where naive area comparisons confound accuracy and ranking [[on-the-evaluation-of-neural-selective-prediction-methods-for-natural-language-pr]].

Threshold discipline separates a policy from a tuned number. The model is frozen, temperature is fit on one calibration half, the operating threshold is set as the score quantile achieving the target risk on the disjoint selection half, and the threshold is locked before the golden set is ever touched. Any re-tuning on golden invalidates the headline [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. The calibration split must be conversation-disjoint and temporal, the most recent window with at least 500 fresh threads, never random-stratified alone, because the exchangeability premise behind conformal-style guarantees fails under the assignment own drift in issues and policy and phrasing [[210600170-adaptive-conformal-inference-under-distribution-shift]]. Split-conformal gives distribution-free coverage bounds on fresh exchangeable points [[a-gentle-introduction-to-conformal-prediction-and-distribution-free-uncertainty]], but exchangeability fails under brand drift, so every coverage-at-risk claim carries an explicit conditional-on-no-further-shift disclaimer. Current workspace has no calibration split. Operating thresholds are NOT MEASURED. The operating risk target of 5 percent intent error on auto-handled items, with sensitivity reported at 1 and 10 percent mirroring published selective question-answering practice, is a starting commitment to be re-fit on brand data, not a theorem [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

Reporting must price abstention, never quote risk alone. The required set is the full risk-coverage curve with area under the curve, area under the generalized risk-coverage curve with default penalty weight for direct comparability with recent methodology that supersedes the older area measure and changes system rankings on most datasets [[240701032-overcoming-common-flaws-in-the-evaluation-of-selective-classification]], selective risk at 70 and 90 percent coverage, coverage at 1 and 5 and 10 percent risk, effective reliability scoring correct as plus one and incorrect as minus one and abstain as zero, recall of actually-answered correct items, fraction of correct predictions needlessly abstained on, post-abstention recovery rate with marginal risk, and the calibration-to-test generalization gap [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. The abstention-price statistics motivate this insistence. State-of-the-art vision models answer under 8 percent of items at 1 percent risk with vanilla thresholds, nearly all correct predictions can sit abstained-on at strict risk targets, and evidence-recovery plus re-attempting abstained items are the proven offsets, lifting coverage by up to 20 points without raising risk at moderate tolerance and improving overall risk by over 20 points in-domain and out-of-domain in question-answering studies [[selective-selective-prediction-reducing-unnecessary-abstention-in-vision-languag]][[post-abstention-towards-reliably-re-attempting-the-abstained-instances-in-qa]]. Operationally, a one-stage retrieval re-attempt with paraphrase re-ask precedes final escalation, accepted only above a second pre-registered entailment bar, with its recovery rate logged as a decision-log entry competitors will lack. Trusted uncertainty frameworks that unify calibration with conformal risk control and beat raw thresholds under shift point the same direction toward learned heads plus explicit risk control rather than a single tuned cutoff [[250901455-trusted-uncertainty-in-large-language-models-a-unified-framework-for-c]]. Online conformal updates with provable long-run coverage under arbitrary drift are the noted streaming extension, deliberately deferred for the one-week static submission [[210600170-adaptive-conformal-inference-under-distribution-shift]].

Every escalation logs a code plus signal values from a five-item taxonomy: low intent confidence, grounding failure, sensitive action or personal-identifier involvement, ambiguity or missing slot, and novelty or out-of-distribution distance [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Confidence alone cannot produce reasons, and the assignment grades decision plus reason. The handoff shows reason plus evidence.
