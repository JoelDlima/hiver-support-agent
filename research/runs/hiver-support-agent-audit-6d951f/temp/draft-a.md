## 1. Problem Framing — What Good and Trustworthy Mean for One Brand

Good for one brand means three verifiable behaviors on future customer messages: the intent label routes to the right playbook, the drafted reply is entailed by how that brand actually resolved similar threads, and the auto-handle versus escalate call matches what a careful human handler would do, with a stated reason every time [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Trustworthy adds the proof layer: those three behaviors are measured on a conversation-disjoint, temporally posterior golden set of 150–250 hand-labelled threads, scored against two honest baselines, with an LLM judge whose agreement with humans is itself measured and gated before any headline is quoted [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]][[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]].

This framing is deliberately narrow. The assignment asks for one brand, not a multi-brand platform, because trust evidence at n=200 only has statistical meaning inside one issue distribution with one voice and one resolution playbook [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. A system that looks good averaged across Apple, Amazon, and an airline can be unsafe on each individually, and the per-label agreement math at n=200 already forces a small taxonomy — there is no headroom to also cover cross-brand variation [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]].

What good explicitly excludes matters as much as what it includes. The agent does not execute actions, issue refunds, change accounts, or call tools. It classifies, drafts, and defers. Any sensitive action, missing slot needed for safe action, or low-confidence grounding escalates with a reason code rather than generating an answer [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. The handoff shows the reason and the evidence, not the suppressed draft, because informing handlers of deferral without surfacing the uncertain guess improves downstream human accuracy, while revealing the low-confidence guess degrades it [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

What was deliberately not built follows from the same logic. No agent framework with tool loops, no multi-step planner, no learned sparse retriever, no dynamic per-query fusion weight, no fine-tuned judge, no synthetic-data-first training set. Each of these has a real ceiling in the literature, but none earns its complexity at subsample scale inside a 15-minute reproduction budget, and each adds a failure surface the golden set is too small to audit [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. The plain pipeline — thread-aware retrieval plus a SetFit-first classifier with routed LLM fallback plus a calibrated escalate gate plus a caged judge — maximizes trust per unit of complexity because every stage has a pre-registered ablation that can remove it if it fails to move a gated metric.

The central forensic claim of this draft, argued without hedging, is that the existing workspace implementation cannot support any trust claim today. Its headline numbers are upper bounds, not deployment estimates, until a thread-aware temporal rebuild is completed [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Everything that follows — taxonomy, architecture, retrieval, escalation, judge — is conditional on that rebuild happening first.

## 2. Data Foundation — Conversation Graph, Brand Selection, Splits, and Leakage Control

The raw Customer Support on Twitter schema carries the grouping key the entire evaluation depends on: each row has `tweet_id`, `author_id`, `inbound`, `created_at`, `text`, plus `response_tweet_id` and `in_response_to_tweet_id` linking turns into threads [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Connected components over that reply graph define `thread_id`, with thread start as the minimum timestamp and per-turn `position_in_thread` and `thread_size` recorded alongside. The TNE-AI conversation derivative preserves this structure explicitly across 794,000 rows and 109 companies [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]], while the 945,000 input-output-pair derivative destroys it by distilling multi-turn threads into isolated normalized pairs — convenient for fine-tuning, unusable for leakage auditing [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Any pipeline step that flattens threads to pairs silently removes the key the split must respect.

Brand selection should be made on submission quality, not row count. Measured per-brand exploratory analysis gives a concrete menu: AmazonHelp contributes roughly 169,840 tweets with 168,823 responses, AppleSupport roughly 106,860 with 106,648, and median first-response latency ranges from about 2.75 minutes for TMobileHelp through 10.73 for AmericanAir and 11.47 for AmazonHelp to 70.97 for AppleSupport and 180.5 for British Airways [[evidence-digest-synthesis-note-placeholder]]. The right pick is the brand whose issue space compresses to six to eight distinguishable intents, whose outbound history contains reusable resolution patterns rather than one-off apologies, whose threads are deep enough to supply context-dependent golden items, and whose escalation opportunity is real — a mix of safely automatable status and how-to turns plus genuinely sensitive, ambiguous, or novel turns [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Volume alone is the wrong criterion; a huge brand with diffuse issues and thin resolutions produces a golden set with unmeasurable per-label agreement.

The forensic audit of the current workspace is severe and specific. The headline Apple inbound pool holds 97,592 rows with columns limited to `tweet_id, author_id, inbound, text, clean` — no `thread_id`, no `created_at`, so conversation-disjoint or temporal splitting is impossible from that artifact [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. The golden file holds 200 rows with columns limited to `text, intent, escalate, escalate_reason` — no tweet, thread, or time keys, so provenance is untraceable and overlap with training, few-shot, or retrieval pools is unauditable by construction [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. All 200 golden texts match pool texts verbatim, confirming the golden set was drawn from the same population that feeds training and retrieval [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. The knowledge base holds 89,694 rows that are 100 percent outbound with tweet identifiers preserved — structurally, it contains the brand's gold responses while golden queries are inbound from the same brand threads — but same-thread target-response containment cannot be checked because neither artifact carries thread identifiers, so the highest-value contamination (the retriever fetching the exact gold answer and the judge rewarding near-perfect groundedness) is currently unmeasurable, which is itself the finding [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. The thread-preserving recipe already exists in the repository for another brand's parquet with `thread_id, created_at, position_in_thread, thread_size, gap_split`, but it was not applied to the headline pipeline [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

Three leakage mechanisms are therefore live, not hypothetical. Same-thread overlap means consecutive turns share entities, phrasing, and resolution state, so a classifier or retriever that saw one turn effectively knows the next [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Retrieval of the target response means the knowledge base, built from brand outbound tweets, contains the gold answer to many evaluation queries [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Future-answering-past means policy, outage status, and links drift across the collection window, so training on later tweets to answer earlier ones is temporal leakage as formally defined: the test set should not contain data from before the training set, and splits should respect dependency structure through block partitioning on threads [[220707048-leakage-and-the-reproducibility-crisis-in-ml-based-science]][[overview-of-leakage-scenarios-in-supervised-machine-learning-journal-of-big-data]]. Measured work on repeated-subject leakage shows performance inflation that worsens on small evaluation sets — exactly the combination present here, where same-thread tweets act as repeated subjects and the golden set holds only 150–250 items [[data-leakage-inflates-prediction-performance-in-connectome-based-machine-learnin]]. Deployment on new threads over time is an out-of-distribution problem, and in-distribution-only numbers do not license deployment claims [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

The rebuild recipe is mechanical and must precede every other measurement. Rebuild the brand slice from raw data preserving linkage columns on every downstream artifact; reconstruct threads as connected components and quarantine orphans from golden eligibility; collapse near-duplicates by normalized-text match plus embedding threshold so template replies do not spray across splits; sort threads by start time and impose a cutoff with knowledge-base, classifier-train, and few-shot material strictly pre-cutoff and golden material strictly post-cutoff, assigning straddling threads by start time; partition every split with group semantics on thread identifiers with a fail-closed zero-overlap assertion executed on every harness run; restrict the knowledge base to pre-cutoff outbound material and additionally exclude same-thread documents at evaluation time, reporting scores with and without that exclusion as a leakage diagnostic; stratify threads rather than tweets within the post-cutoff window by weak-intent bucket, escalate flag, and thread-size bucket, backfilling rare strata only from the latest pre-cutoff threads with per-stratum disclosure; and stamp every golden row with tweet, thread, timestamp, text, intent, escalate flag, reason, and stratum [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Group-aware splitting on thread identifiers is the standard mechanism for this design [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]], and time-ordered evaluation with a boundary gap is the standard guard against training on the future to predict the past [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Until that recipe is executed, every number in the workspace must be labeled an upper bound.

## 3. Intent Taxonomy — Deriving a Small Routable Measurable Label Scheme

The taxonomy is squeezed from both ends, which is why six to eight intents plus an explicit out-of-scope class is the committed size. From the model side, controlled experiments show LLM out-of-scope detection degrades faster than encoder performance as label scope broadens and label count grows, with the direct guidance that fine-grained labels and smaller label spaces favor LLM out-of-scope handling [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. From the annotation side, a 200-item golden set with a 20-per-intent minimum imposes an absolute ceiling of ten intents, and once decision, difficulty, and temporal strata with their own minimums are added, the practical ceiling falls to about seven [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. When model behavior and annotation arithmetic independently point to the same narrow band, the size is over-determined rather than stylistic.

Banking77's 77 intents over 10,003 training and 3,080 test queries of clean single-domain banking English are the counter-model, not the template [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]]. Seventy-seven overlapping classes maximize confusion, prompt-length failure, and per-label data starvation at golden-set scale. The locus evidence on synthetic augmentation reinforces the point: GPT-generated training data boosts accuracy only when intents are sufficiently distinct, and with semantically close intents the generator produces utterances belonging to neighbor intents [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. A brand taxonomy with pairs like delayed versus lost versus damaged, or billing versus refund, sits exactly in the failure regime where synthetic data cross-contaminates. Banking77 is therefore legitimately used for intent-method prototyping only — calibrating the SetFit-versus-LLM harness, tuning the router, validating the augmentation sweet spot — and never to inflate headline numbers, which must come solely from the brand's own threaded golden set.

Derivation must be data-driven and documented. The defensible sequence is: embed a thread-aware sample with a Twitter-pretrained encoder, cluster, inspect clusters against actual threads (not isolated tweets), draft candidate intents with one-line scope definitions written to exclude neighbors, pilot-label 30 items, measure pairwise confusion, and merge any pair exceeding roughly 15 percent cross-confusion [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. That merge rule is the enforcement mechanism that keeps the scheme routable: if two intents cannot be told apart by trained annotators at pilot scale, no classifier routing on them can be trusted, and no escalation policy conditioned on them is meaningful. The one-line scope definitions plus two to three boundary examples per intent, with label names masked to neutral identifiers during LLM prompting to prevent spurious name memorization, follow the controlled protocol that produced the load-bearing hybrid numbers [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]].

Concretely, a brand agent of this shape typically converges on intents like account-access and authentication failure, billing-charge-refund dispute, order-status and delivery tracking, device-or-service fault report, plan-change and upgrade inquiry, complaint-escalation and callback request, plus explicit out-of-scope-other (NOT MEASURED for this brand until the rebuild's clustering pass is executed — listed here as the expected shape, not a finding). Each intent needs a resolution-playbook pointer: which historical outbound patterns resolve it, which slots are required before safe auto-handling, and which variants always escalate. Intents without distinct resolutions are not intents for this assignment; they are topic labels, and topic labels do not route.

The out-of-scope class deserves special emphasis because every model struggles with it. Out-of-scope recall at best-F1 is poor across both LLM and encoder families in the controlled tables, with encoder baselines near 0.38 average and the best LLMs near 0.60–0.64 [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. Negative augmentation — removing or random-string-replacing keywords in a fifth of training sentences and treating the results as out-of-scope — lifts the encoder substantially and replicates across datasets [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. That augmentation is therefore part of the committed classifier recipe, while GPT synthetic generation is capped at a tiny ablation of at most seven synthetic items per intent, because beyond that point real-data comparisons show degradation and the bleed mechanism is worst on exactly the close-intent pairs a support taxonomy contains [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]][[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]].

## 4. System Architecture — Minimal Design That Maximizes Trust per Complexity

The committed architecture is a plain pipeline with no agent framework, and the framework rejection is load-bearing rather than aesthetic. Current-generation LangChain ships stable with hundreds of integrations and earns its overhead for multi-step agent workflows with tool loops, but practitioner consensus across independent sources is that plain Python with direct API calls is faster to build, debug, and maintain for single-pass retrieval-augmented generation [[evidence-digest-synthesis-note-placeholder]]. This submission is single-pass by design: classify, retrieve thread-window context, draft once, gate once, with a single pre-registered re-attempt before final escalation. There is no planning loop, no tool choice, no reflection cycle that would justify a graph orchestrator. Every additional abstraction layer would need its own golden-set evidence at n=200 with 6–8 point minimum detectable effects, and none can supply it.

The pipeline has four stages with one shared confidence doctrine. First, a SetFit-first intent classifier on a Twitter-pretrained backbone with cheap negative augmentation routes only uncertain items to a low-cost instruction model with retrieval-filtered dynamic few-shot examples [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]][[bertweet-a-pre-trained-language-model-for-english-tweets-acl-anthology]]. Second, a hybrid BM25-plus-dense retriever over thread-window chunks with reciprocal-rank fusion cold-start, cross-validated weighting once labels exist, and cross-encoder rerank of at most fifty candidates supplies the top five to ten grounded contexts [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]]. Third, a reply generator drafts strictly from retrieved brand history with an entailment gate that can veto auto-handling independently of intent confidence [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Fourth, an escalation policy built on temperature-scaled maximum softmax probability plus that entailment gate, with a learned calibrator admitted only on proof of cross-split wins, makes the final auto-handle call with a five-code reason on every escalation [[200809371-towards-improving-selective-prediction-ability-of-nlp-systems]][[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]].

The classifier choice is the most argued stage, so the numbers need stating precisely. On six real task-oriented-dialogue datasets, the SetFit baseline averages 0.600 F1 at 0.030 seconds per query, negative augmentation lifts it above 0.65, the best native LLM averages 0.736 at 1.7 seconds, and the uncertainty-routed hybrid lands within about two points of the native LLM outside distribution shift (about four points including the hardest shift slice) at roughly half the latency while routing only a third to under half of queries to the LLM [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. SetFit runs roughly fifty-six times faster than the best LLM per query [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]]. The cost companion result is equally stark: retrieval-filtered dynamic few-shot with the top twenty neighbors reaches 85.5 percent at 42 dollars where a full 231-example classic prompt reaches 83.1 percent at 740 dollars on the same 3,080-query test set — cheaper and better simultaneously, because the classic approach hits context and cost walls that dynamic filtering sidesteps [[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]]. Those figures come from clean banking and shopping dialogue rather than noisy tweets, so they must not be quoted as Twitter proof; the transfer gap is closed by swapping the generic paraphrase backbone for a Twitter-pretrained encoder, since the first large-scale tweet model with the standard base architecture trained under the robust pretraining procedure beats generic strong baselines on tweet part-of-speech, entity, and classification tasks [[bertweet-a-pre-trained-language-model-for-english-tweets-acl-anthology]].

Routing mechanics carry a mandatory contest. Monte Carlo dropout as a Bayesian approximation — sampling multiple stochastic forward passes and treating prediction variance as uncertainty — is the hybrid's router, but its variational character typically underestimates uncertainty and raw softmax outputs must never be read as confidence [[dropout-as-a-bayesian-approximation-representing-model-uncertainty-in-deep-learn]]. The large-scale selective-prediction study across seventeen datasets finds that no method, including Monte Carlo dropout and learned calibrators, consistently and considerably beats the simplest maximum-probability baseline across in-domain, out-of-domain, and adversarial settings, with dropout winning duplicate detection but failing natural-language inference especially out-of-domain [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]]. The architecture therefore treats full-layer Monte Carlo dropout with ten passes as the default router while requiring it to beat temperature-scaled maximum probability on area under the risk-coverage curve on both held-out and temporal slices before it governs either routing or escalation. A narrower study shows full-layer dropout can beat maximum probability substantially on in-domain text-classification rejection curves, which is why dropout stays the challenger rather than being discarded [[evidence-digest-synthesis-note-placeholder]].

Storage and serving stay embedded and reproducible. At one-brand subsample scale of tens to low hundreds of thousands of chunks, every credible store answers top-ten queries in single-digit to tens of milliseconds, with in-process libraries fastest and managed services adding operational surface for no measurable gain at this n [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. The committed choice is in-process BM25 plus a local dense index with reranking, versioned indices, and per-stage rank logging, keeping the 15-minute reproduction path free of containers and clusters. Data engineering uses columnar frames and an embedded analytical store for the multi-million-row thread rebuild, consistent with measured 10–100× speedups over naive frame code on million-row pipelines [[evidence-digest-synthesis-note-placeholder]]. Support prompts carry redaction, injection guards, and escalation paths as non-negotiable, since unguarded support pipelines leak personally identifying information and obey injected instructions inside graded content [[evidence-digest-synthesis-note-placeholder]].

## 5. Retrieval and Reply Generation — Grounding Replies in Historical Resolutions

Retrieval is the grounding bottleneck: whatever the retriever fails to return, the generator cannot truthfully say. Pure dense retrieval is the worst default on support tweets for a structural reason agreed across every retrieval source. A bi-encoder pools all token representations into one fixed vector, which buys paraphrase generalization but destroys the lexical identity of rare strings — an error code becomes a vector about errors in general rather than the document containing that exact string, while an inverted index either finds the term or does not, with rarity helping rather than hurting [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. Twitter support concentrates every dense-hostile feature inside a few hundred characters: handles, hashtags, order and tracking numbers, version strings, URLs, typos, abbreviations, and two-to-five-word queries with almost no semantic content [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Cross-domain zero-shot precedent shows dense models trained on web passages frequently failing to beat BM25 outside their training distribution, which is why serious teams treat dense as a complement rather than a replacement [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]].

The failure is silent, which is what makes it trust-critical. A dense miss still returns something, the language model then writes a fluent confident reply with wrong specifics, no error fires, and evaluation sets built from accepted production answers systematically exclude exactly these cases — so blended recall can look acceptable while identifier-stratum recall sits near forty percent (ESTIMATED magnitude from practitioner diagnosis literature; brand-stratum recall NOT MEASURED until the stratified ablation runs) [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]]. That confident, internally consistent, wrong-about-specifics shape is the hallucination pattern that kills trust in grounded reply generation.

The committed retrieval stack answers it directly. Index overlapping thread windows — the target turn plus one to two neighbors, or whole short threads within the token budget — rather than isolated tweets, each prepended with roughly fifty to one hundred tokens of context carrying brand, thread identifier, turn position, a one-line issue summary, and the prior turn [[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]]. That contextualization is not cosmetic: contextual embeddings cut top-twenty retrieval failures by about thirty-five percent, adding contextual BM25 pushes the cut to about forty-nine percent, and adding reranking pushes it to about sixty-seven percent in cross-domain experiments [[contextual-retrieval-in-ai-systems-anthropic]]. The BM25 arm must preserve the signal dense destroys — handles, tags, digits, hyphens, slashes, version strings — with light stemming and short-document length normalization, while the dense arm uses a short-text-friendly encoder. Fuse one hundred candidates per arm with reciprocal-rank fusion at the conventional constant for cold start, then migrate to a cross-validated single-weight blend once the golden set yields at least forty relevance pairs, expecting a sparse-heavy weight near 0.3 for identifier-loaded support traffic but verifying by sweep rather than asserting it [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. Deduplicate with maximal-marginal-relevance so near-duplicate adjacent turns do not crowd out diverse evidence, rerank at most thirty to fifty candidates with a cross-encoder, and pass the top five to ten into the generator with per-stage ranks logged [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]].

Controlled deltas justify the complexity if and only if the brand-data ablation confirms them. An enterprise tech-docs stack moves from 0.67 recall-at-ten dense-only to 0.78 hybrid to 0.84 with reranking — roughly thirty percent stacked over pure dense [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. A furniture-catalog benchmark moves naive rank fusion only about a point over the best single arm but reaches seven-plus percent with tuned weighting and field boosting [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]]. Practitioner synthesis places the hybrid lift at eight to fifteen percent concentrated almost entirely on exact-term queries [[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. The pre-registered gate for this submission is therefore a five-point identifier-stratum recall-at-ten gain without blended regression and within latency budget; if the stratified ablation fails that gate, hybrid plus rerank is documented over-engineering and the recommendation reverts to a simpler stage. Rerank latency discipline is strict: thirty to fifty candidates cost roughly one to two hundred milliseconds total, and needing two hundred or more signals broken first-stage recall rather than a rerank deficit [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]].

Reply generation itself is constrained generation, not open chat. The generator sees only retrieved brand history plus the current thread window, drafts the minimal next response consistent with the brand playbook, and every actionable claim must be entailed by cited retrieved context — any unsupported claim caps groundedness and any invented policy, compensation, timeline, or echoed personal identifier fails the safety dimension outright [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. A second independent signal, natural-language-inference entailment of the draft by the retrieved exemplars with a pre-registered threshold near 0.9, gates auto-handling regardless of intent confidence: low entailment always escalates as grounding failure [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Generation and judging model families must differ, since the judge's self-preference mechanism is structural rather than instruction-removable (see Section 7). Graded content is wrapped as untrusted with injection guards, formatting is normalized before judging, and the generator's latency dominates end-to-end time so rerank cost is justified only through measured precision movement.

## 6. Escalation Policy — Automating Only What Is Safe to Automate

Escalation is a selective-prediction problem with a coverage price, and the literature forces a two-signal floor. The primary signal is temperature-scaled maximum softmax probability of the intent head: modern networks are systematically overconfident with mean confidence substantially exceeding accuracy, and single-parameter temperature scaling fit on held-out likelihood is the most effective post-hoc fix on most vision and document-classification sets [[supplementary-materials-foron-calibration-of-modern-neural-networks]]. The mandatory conjunction is the entailment gate above — intent confidence never licenses auto-handling of an ungrounded draft. A learned confidence-plus-difficulty calibrator of the published form, which reports double-digit risk-coverage gains over maximum probability on inference and duplicate-detection tasks [[200809371-towards-improving-selective-prediction-ability-of-nlp-systems]], is admitted only if it beats scaled maximum probability on both area under the risk-coverage curve and a ranking-quality refinement metric on held-out and temporal slices alike, because the larger follow-up study shows those gains do not transfer reliably across tasks and settings [[investigating-selective-prediction-approaches-across-several-tasks-in-iid-ood-an]]. Monotone scaling improves calibration error and threshold interpretability but cannot repair ranking, so ordering quality must be measured separately before any learned signal is trusted [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

Threshold discipline is what separates a policy from a tuned number. The model is frozen, temperature is fit on one calibration half, the operating threshold is set as the score quantile achieving the target risk on the disjoint selection half, and the threshold is locked before the golden set is ever touched — any re-tuning on golden invalidates the headline [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. The calibration split must be conversation-disjoint and temporal (the most recent window, at least 500 fresh threads; NOT MEASURED — current workspace has no calibration split), never random-stratified alone, because the exchangeability premise behind conformal-style guarantees fails under the assignment's own drift in issues, policy, and phrasing [[210600170-adaptive-conformal-inference-under-distribution-shift]]. The operating risk target of five percent intent error on auto-handled items, with sensitivity reported at one and ten percent mirroring published selective visual-question-answering practice, is a starting commitment to be re-fit on brand data, not a theorem [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

Reporting must price abstention, never quote risk alone. The required set is the full risk-coverage curve with area under the curve, area under the generalized risk-coverage curve with default penalty weight for direct comparability with recent selective-classification methodology that supersedes the older area measure and changes system rankings on most datasets [[240701032-overcoming-common-flaws-in-the-evaluation-of-selective-classification]], selective risk at seventy and ninety percent coverage, coverage at one, five, and ten percent risk, effective reliability scoring correct as plus one, incorrect as minus one, and abstain as zero, recall of actually-answered correct items, fraction of correct predictions needlessly abstained on, post-abstention recovery rate with marginal risk, and the calibration-to-test generalization gap [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. The abstention-price statistics motivate this insistence: state-of-the-art vision models answer under eight percent of items at one percent risk with vanilla thresholds, nearly all correct predictions can sit abstained-on at strict risk targets, and evidence-recovery plus re-attempting abstained items are the proven offsets, lifting coverage by up to twenty points without raising risk at moderate tolerance and improving overall risk by over twenty points in-domain and out-of-domain in question-answering studies [[selective-selective-prediction-reducing-unnecessary-abstention-in-vision-languag]][[post-abstention-towards-reliably-re-attempting-the-abstained-instances-in-qa]]. Operationally, a one-stage retrieval re-attempt with paraphrase re-ask precedes final escalation, accepted only above a second pre-registered entailment bar, with its recovery rate logged as a decision-log entry competitors will lack.

Every escalation logs a code plus signal values from a five-item taxonomy: low intent confidence, grounding failure, sensitive action or personal-identifier involvement, ambiguity or missing slot, and novelty or out-of-distribution distance [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Confidence alone cannot produce reasons, and the assignment grades decision plus reason. Trusted uncertainty frameworks that unify calibration with conformal risk control and beat raw thresholds under shift point the same direction — learned heads plus explicit risk control rather than a single tuned cutoff — and online conformal updates with provable long-run coverage under arbitrary drift are the noted streaming extension, deliberately deferred for the one-week static submission [[250901455-trusted-uncertainty-in-large-language-models-a-unified-framework-for-c]][[210600170-adaptive-conformal-inference-under-distribution-shift]].

## 7. Evaluation Baselines and the LLM Judge — Proving It Works

Two baselines are honest, not strawmen, because each isolates one load-bearing claim. The trivial baseline — majority-intent plus fixed-template reply plus always-escalate on anything non-trivial — scores the floor every smarter stage must clear on its own metric: intent accuracy and macro-F1, groundedness, and escalation precision-recall with coverage. The simple baseline — TF-IDF or BM25 nearest-neighbor intent plus direct-LLM draft without thread-window retrieval plus raw-threshold escalate — tests whether the three complexity bets (Twitter-backbone SetFit with routing, hybrid-plus-rerank thread-window retrieval, scaled-plus-entailment escalation) each move their gated metric. If the full system cannot beat the simple baseline by more than the golden set's minimum detectable effect of roughly six to eight points at n=200, the complexity is unjustified and the report must say so [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. All baseline scores are NOT MEASURED until the leakage-safe rebuild; current workspace comparisons are uninterpretable because both arms train and retrieve from contaminated pools.

The golden set is the artifact the headlines stand on, so its certification gates headlines absolutely. The committed spec is 200 hand-labelled threads: 120 randomly sampled within strata plus 80 targeted hard cases covering short, ambiguous, multi-intent, sarcasm and negation, identifier-heavy, and context-dependent multi-turn items, with minimums per intent (at least twenty), per decision (sixty to eighty escalations so both classes have stable estimates), per difficulty (the random-hard split), and per time (full timeline plus a recent-month drift slice), every item carrying intent, reference resolution note, escalate flag with reason, and stratum, labelled under a one-page ordered decision procedure with a 30-item pilot revising the guideline before main labelling and full double-to-triple independent labelling throughout [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Agreement gates use the label-type-correct machinery: nominal intent governed by Krippendorff's alpha with pairwise and multi-rater coefficients as secondary, ordinal reply-quality dimensions by weighted agreement with rank correlation alongside, binary escalation by chance-corrected agreement plus F1 against adjudicated gold with prevalence disclosed [[measuring-inter-rater-reliability-for-nominal-data-which-coefficients-and-confid]][[221013265-toward-improved-inference-for-krippendorffs-alpha-agreement-coefficien]]. The ship bar for the gold artifact itself is alpha at or above 0.80 with the confidence-interval lower bound at or above 0.70, no label below 0.60, prevalence-robust supplements reported for rare labels; the 0.667–0.80 band means tentative conclusions only with guideline repair and re-annotation, never headlines, and below 0.667 blocks and forces a specification rewrite [[krippendorffs-alpha-intercoder-reliability-casrai]][[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Those cutoffs are risk-calibrated choices rather than theorems, with interval widths of five to ten points even at hundreds of items and under-dispersed naive bootstraps at small n favoring analytical jackknife intervals at n=200 [[221013265-toward-improved-inference-for-krippendorffs-alpha-agreement-coefficien]][[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. The sarcasm stratum is reported separately and never averaged away, because author-intended sarcasm studies show third-party pairwise agreement near 0.36–0.39 with large intended-versus-perceived gaps — the slice legitimately lives far below the global bar and reflects task subjectivity rather than annotator failure [[isarcasm-a-dataset-of-intended-sarcasm]]. No bare majority votes: every disagreement routes to a named reviewer whose one-sentence rationale becomes a dated guideline-changelog entry, full soft-label distributions are preserved, and rotating honeypot gold tasks monitor compliance while a disagreement-distribution audit monitors well-formedness [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]].

The judge protocol is a caged pointwise instrument, and the cage is the entire argument. Pointwise anchored scoring — three five-point dimensions with few-shot anchors at 1, 3, and 5 (groundedness as entailment by cited brand context with any unsupported actionable claim capped, resolution-usefulness as the correct next step or channel per brand playbook with generic deflection capped, safety-honesty as no invented policy or identifier echo with uncertainty qualified) plus a separate categorical escalate verdict emitted as its own structured field — kills position bias by construction [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. That construction choice is now evidence-backed over the older pairwise-default wisdom: the newest multi-family controlled study finds position effects negligible at or below 0.04 on current models while style and format effects dominate at 0.10–0.76, confirmed as bias rather than readability by human checks [[judging-the-judges-a-systematic-evaluation-of-bias-mitigation-strategies-in-llm]]. The older headline that a third of pairwise verdicts flip on order swap remains true for pairwise designs and is exactly why pairwise is reserved for close-call system comparison with dual-order aggregation and consistency reporting, never for grading clear-cut gold items where swap-averaging discards correct verdicts [[llm-judge-biases-position-verbosity-self-preference-aitldr]][[llm-as-a-judge-pitfalls-bias-detection-mitigation-aitldr]].

Each remaining bias gets a structural rather than prompt-only fix, because prompt instruction alone does not remove the structural ones. Self-preference is perplexity-familiarity — judges over-rate low-perplexity familiar text whether or not they wrote it — quantified at 0.52 recall-gap bias for the strongest proprietary judge with demographic-parity variant near 0.75, so the judge family must differ from every generator family under test and the self-preference gap must be audited below 0.10 with gold-anchored scoring that disentangles liking one's style from writing better outputs [[self-preference-bias-in-llm-as-a-judge]]. Verbosity is heterogeneous across judges rather than uniform — some prefer longer, one prefers concise, one is near-neutral — so per-judge length-score correlation must be measured below 0.15 with explicit anti-verbosity and anti-format clauses, formatting normalized pre-judge, and any pairwise win-rate headline additionally passing length-controlled regression of the form that lifted arena correlation from 0.94 to 0.98 [[length-controlled-alpacaevala-simple-way-to-debias-automatic-evaluators]][[judging-the-judges-a-systematic-evaluation-of-bias-mitigation-strategies-in-llm]]. Sycophancy is met by requiring cited textual evidence before the score plus rubric language penalizing unsupported confidence below qualified correctness [[llm-as-a-judge-pitfalls-bias-detection-mitigation-aitldr]]. Generation-style auto-rated evaluation with chain-of-thought reaches mid-0.50s rank correlation with humans on summarization and higher on dialogue and factual-consistency tasks, beating prior reference-free metrics [[g-eval-nlg-evaluation-using-gpt-4-with-better-human-alignment]] — the proof that a pinned judge can work — while the caution that reference-free metrics are formally one generation model evaluating another, test-time optimizable and at times anti-human, is why RAGAS-style faithfulness stays a secondary diagnostic and triage signal, preferably via a non-LLM entailment variant spot-checked against humans, never the headline [[on-the-limitations-of-reference-free-evaluations-of-generated-text-acl-anthology]][[benchmarking-llm-faithfulness-in-rag-with-evolving-leaderboards]].

Promotion gates before any judge headline is quoted are therefore tiered and human-first: human-human agreement passes first with at least twenty percent double annotation, then judge-human agreement must clear per-dimension weighted agreement near 0.60, escalation agreement near 0.65, raw agreement at or above seventy-five percent, pairwise-arm swap consistency at or above 0.80, length-score correlation bounded, and self-preference gap bounded, all with bootstrap intervals and a pre-registered minimum detectable effect, with system comparisons by paired significance tests with multiple-comparison correction [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]][[241115594-a-survey-on-llm-as-a-judge]]. The commonly cited eighty-percent human-agreement figure is an aggregate benchmark number, not a per-task production guarantee [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Absolute pointwise scoring is preferred over pairwise for the headline precisely because controlled feedback-protocol comparisons show pairwise flipping an order of magnitude more often than absolute scoring under distractors [[pairwise-or-pointwise-evaluating-feedback-protocols-for-bias-in-llm-based-evalua]]. Disagreement analysis closes the loop: stratified confusion by dimension, intent, and action type, per-class agreement on the escalate minority, and critique review of twenty to thirty mismatches classified as guideline gap, judge bias, or genuine ambiguity feeding back into anchors, re-audited on schedule with rotation on drift below seventy-five percent.

## 8. Failure Modes Headline-Number Critique and Limits

Five failure modes, each with the shape the golden set must contain, lead the critique because the report is required to show them with real examples (example slots listed as NOT MEASURED until golden labelling completes; root causes and mitigations committed now).

First, same-thread retrieval contamination producing perfect-looking groundedness: the retriever returns the query's own gold response from the knowledge base, the generator copies it, the judge rewards entailment, and the headline looks excellent while measuring memorization. Expected: a paraphrase-grounded draft scored on retrieved neighbors. Actual in the unrebuilt workspace: potentially verbatim gold scored as grounded (NOT MEASURED whether it occurred — unauditable by construction). Root cause: thread-sprayed pools with no exclusion. Mitigation: pre-cutoff knowledge base plus same-thread exclusion with with-and-without diagnostic reporting [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

Second, identifier-hallucinated replies on order, tracking, version, and error-code queries: fluent, confident, wrong specifics with no error firing. Expected: exact-ID correctness via the BM25 arm. Actual: semantic-neighbor substitution from dense-only retrieval. Root cause: pooled embeddings averaging away rare-token identity [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[why-dense-retrieval-fails-on-acronyms-ids-and-code-and-how-hybrid-fixes-it-pradh]]. Mitigation: hybrid-plus-rerank gated on the identifier-stratum ablation plus exact-ID downstream scoring.

Third, confident auto-handles on sarcasm, negation, and multi-intent turns: "great, my phone is totally fixed (not)" routed as praise and auto-closed. Expected: escalation for ambiguity. Actual: high maximum-probability on the wrong single intent. Root cause: single-label taxonomy pressure plus temperature scaling that calibrates but does not reorder [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]][[isarcasm-a-dataset-of-intended-sarcasm]]. Mitigation: multi-intent flag as ambiguity-escalation, sarcasm stratum in golden with separate reporting, margin and entropy features in the challenger calibrator.

Fourth, judge-rewarded verbosity and polish over correctness: a long markdown-formatted deflection outscores a terse correct next step. Expected: concise-correct winning. Actual: style and length effects dominating content. Root cause: RLHF-inherited thoroughness heuristic plus format bias now measured as the dominant judge skew [[judging-the-judges-a-systematic-evaluation-of-bias-mitigation-strategies-in-llm]][[llm-judge-biases-position-verbosity-self-preference-aitldr]]. Mitigation: formatting normalization, anti-verbosity and anti-format rubric clauses, per-judge length audit, length-controlled win-rate analysis [[length-controlled-alpacaevala-simple-way-to-debias-automatic-evaluators]].

Fifth, temporal staleness: outage, policy, or link advice correct at training time and wrong at evaluation time, delivered confidently. Expected: escalation on novelty or grounding failure. Actual: interpolation from a random split that mixed eras. Root cause: future-answering-past leakage plus missing novelty signal [[220707048-leakage-and-the-reproducibility-crisis-in-ml-based-science]][[210600170-adaptive-conformal-inference-under-distribution-shift]]. Mitigation: temporal golden window, novelty distance feature, explicit exchangeability disclaimer on every coverage-at-risk claim.

What is misleading about the headline number, stated directly. Any accuracy, groundedness, or risk-coverage figure computed on the current random tweet-level split overstates deployment performance by an unknown amount through three compounding channels: correlated same-thread turns shared across train and test, gold answers present in the retrieval index, and future information answering past queries — with small-evaluation-set inflation amplifying all three [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]][[data-leakage-inflates-prediction-performance-in-connectome-based-machine-learnin]]. Blended retrieval recall hides identifier-stratum collapse; blended judge agreement hides escalate-minority and sarcasm-slice failure; risk without coverage hides the abstention price; reference-free faithfulness without human anchoring hides judge-on-judge circularity [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[on-the-limitations-of-reference-free-evaluations-of-generated-text-acl-anthology]]. Sub-six-point system gaps at n=200 are noise inside the confidence intervals, not wins [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]].

Source Tensions discussion, engaged explicitly rather than averaged. On style versus position bias, the newest controlled multi-family evidence ranks style an order of magnitude above position while older pairwise literature documents large swap-flip rates — both true, because they measure different designs: position dominates pairwise prompts, style dominates everything including pointwise, so the committed portfolio leads with deterministic style neutralization (normalization plus rubric plus length-controlled analysis) and keeps swap auditing as verification rather than the headline mitigation [[judging-the-judges-a-systematic-evaluation-of-bias-mitigation-strategies-in-llm]][[llm-judge-biases-position-verbosity-self-preference-aitldr]]. On SetFit versus native LLM, few-shot prompting wins raw accuracy with zero training while the routed hybrid wins the deployable trade — the fight resolves conditionally: SetFit-first governs unless the brand-data contest shows the route rate needed to match LLM accuracy exceeding roughly sixty percent or Twitter-backbone gains vanishing [[intent-detection-in-the-age-of-llms]][[making-llms-worth-every-penny-resource-limited-text-classification-in-banking-ac]]. On hybrid versus dense-only, silent-failure mechanism plus three stacked controlled deltas favor hybrid while naive fusion gains near one point and operational costs are real — resolved by the five-point identifier-gated ablation with a documented revert path [[hybrid-search-in-production-why-bm25-still-wins-on-the-queries-that-matter]][[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. On the caged judge versus human-only scoring, correlations near 0.5–0.8 prove a pinned judge can work while structural self-preference and reference-free circularity prove a bare judge proves nothing — resolved by tiered human-first gates with RAGAS secondary [[g-eval-nlg-evaluation-using-gpt-4-with-better-human-alignment]][[self-preference-bias-in-llm-as-a-judge]][[on-the-limitations-of-reference-free-evaluations-of-generated-text-acl-anthology]]. ## 9. Reproducibility Security Scalability and One-Week Roadmap

Reproducibility is a graded deliverable, so the design treats the 15-minute path as a constraint, not an aspiration. The repository pins dependencies, seeds, and model checkpoints, versions both indices with build scripts and freshness checks, and ships a single entry script that rebuilds the thread-aware subsample, runs both baselines plus the candidate, executes the fail-closed overlap assertions, and prints every headline with confidence intervals from a frozen subsample that evaluators can run without network access beyond model download (NOT MEASURED end-to-end runtime until the rebuild lands; the in-process store choice keeps the retrieval stages in milliseconds so the language-model calls dominate) [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Prompt texts, judge rubrics with anchors, few-shot examples, calibration thresholds, and the guideline changelog are versioned artifacts, not inline strings, so any headline can be traced to the exact configuration that produced it. Evaluation-harness build-versus-buy is decided for code-first: hosted experiment trackers offer real dataset versioning and regression gating, but a dependency-free harness with logged per-stage ranks and scores is auditable in minutes and has no service credential path to break during evaluation.

Security and privacy are escalation-adjacent controls, not afterthoughts. Personally identifying information is redacted before indexing, retrieval, judging, and logging; prompts wrap graded content as untrusted with explicit do-not-obey handling; secrets live outside the repository with rotation documented; and the sensitive-action reason code forces human handling of refunds, account changes, financial and health identifiers, and suspected injection or repeated-refusal cases [[evidence-digest-synthesis-note-placeholder]]. Scalability arguments are kept honest at subsample scale. ## 10. Opinionated Synthesis — Committed Recommendations and Decision Log

The committed thesis, stated once without qualification stacking: treat every workspace number as an upper bound until the thread-aware temporal rebuild; ship SetFit-first with full-layer Monte Carlo-dropout routing on a Twitter-pretrained backbone; retrieve with hybrid BM25-plus-dense fusion and cross-encoder rerank over thread-window chunks gated on a five-point identifier gain; judge with a caged pointwise instrument behind tiered human-first gates and keep reference-free scores secondary; escalate on scaled maximum-probability plus an entailment gate with a learned calibrator admitted only on cross-split wins, reporting the generalized risk-coverage area; build it as a plain pipeline with six to eight intents; and execute in the order above because each step's evidence is conditional on the prior step's artifact.
