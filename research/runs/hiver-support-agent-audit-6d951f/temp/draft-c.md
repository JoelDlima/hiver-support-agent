## 1. Problem Framing — What Good and Trustworthy Mean for One Brand

For this assignment good has a narrow, testable meaning. For one brand, an incoming customer message must be routed to the right action, answered only with what brand history actually supports, and escalated whenever safe automation is not possible — with a reason a reviewer can audit. Trustworthy means that claim survives an adversarial reading of the numbers behind it. The system is secondary. The proof is the product.

That framing matters because the existing workspace implementation cannot be trusted as evidence. Forensic inspection found the headline Apple pipeline stores an inbound pool of 97,592 rows with only `tweet_id, author_id, inbound, text, clean` and no `thread_id` or `created_at`, a golden set of 200 rows with only `text, intent, escalate, escalate_reason` and no identifiers or timestamps, 200 of 200 golden texts matching pool texts verbatim, and a knowledge base of 89,694 outbound rows that structurally contains the brand's own gold responses while same-thread containment cannot even be checked for lack of keys [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. A thread-preserving artifact (`virgin_threads.parquet` with `thread_id, created_at, position_in_thread, thread_size, gap_split`) exists in the repository but was not applied to the headline brand [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Under standard leakage definitions — test data must not predate training data, and splits must respect dependency structure [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]] — the current numbers are upper bounds, not deployment estimates. Every recommendation that follows therefore starts from a rebuild, not a tune-up.

The central thesis of this draft is that trust per unit complexity is maximized by a minimal, dependency-ordered system: conversation-disjoint temporal splits first, then strata derived from those splits, then ablations that consume those strata, then gates that certify the ablation winners. Splits → strata → ablations → gates is the spine. Anything that breaks the order — a judge score without certified gold, a retrieval win without thread exclusion, an escalation threshold tuned on the test set — reintroduces the exact optimism the assignment asks candidates to expose.

What good means therefore decomposes into four independently measured contracts. Intent classification is good when 6–8 mutually distinguishable intents route correctly on future threads, with out-of-scope detection that degrades gracefully rather than forcing a wrong label. Reply generation is good when every actionable claim in a draft is entailed by cited retrieved brand history, the next step matches brand playbook (channel, link, ask), and no policy, compensation, timeline, or personal data is invented. Escalation is good when the auto-handled slice meets a pre-registered risk target and every escalate carries a coded reason plus evidence, not just a score. Reproducibility is good when a stranger reproduces the headline in under 15 minutes from a subsample without special hardware. Deliberately not built: multi-brand generality, live channel integration, agent tool loops, learned sparse retrieval, dynamic per-query fusion weights, fine-tuned generation, and any claim about full 3M-row scale — each is documented as a deferred option with the measurement that would revive it.

Boundary condition on the whole framing: if the submission claimed only "interpolation accuracy on the frozen 2017 snapshot," thread-disjoint random splits with retrieval exclusion would suffice. The assignment demands more — "good enough to trust" on future messages — so the headline slice must be temporally posterior and the report must show the calibration-to-test generalization gap explicitly. Deployment on new threads over time is an out-of-distribution problem by definition [[211011334-generalized-out-of-distribution-detection-a-survey]], and standard metrics computed on interpolating splits do not license deployment claims [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

## 2. Data Foundation — Conversation Graph, Brand Selection, Splits, and Leakage Control

The raw Customer Support on Twitter data is not a table of independent tweets. Each row carries `tweet_id`, anonymized `author_id`, `inbound` flag, `created_at`, `text` with masked private data, plus `in_response_to_tweet_id` and `response_tweet_id` link fields that reconstruct entire threads [[customer-support-on-twitter-openbigdataorg]]. The corpus covers major brands such as Apple, Amazon, Uber, Delta, and Spotify with roughly 516 MB of CSV [[customer-support-on-twitter-openbigdataorg]], widely mirrored as the ~3M-row thoughtvector source [[github-amruta33customer-support-on-twitter-the-customer-support-on-twitter-datas]]. Any step that flattens threads to isolated pairs silently removes the grouping key the evaluation must respect.

Two derivatives illustrate the choice. The TNE-AI conversation build preserves threading explicitly as `conversation_id, company, conversation` across 794,000 rows and 109 companies [[tne-aicustomer-support-on-twitter-conversation-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. The 945,000-row input-output derivative does the opposite: it distills multi-turn threads into normalized input-output pairs with slang, emoji, URL, and mention cleaning [[mohammadothmanmo-customer-support-tweets-945k-datasets-at-hugging-face]]. The latter is convenient for fine-tuning and useless for leakage auditing, because the thread identifier is gone. The implementable rule is blunt: never adopt a pair-flattened derivative as the primary source. Rebuild from raw `twcs.csv` and carry identifiers forward.

Brand selection should maximize submission quality, not row count. Per-brand exploratory analysis on the exact corpus reports AmazonHelp at 169,840 tweets and 168,823 responses with median response 11.47 minutes, AppleSupport at 106,860 tweets and 106,648 responses with median 70.97 minutes, TMobileHelp at 34,317 tweets and 34,229 responses with median 2.75 minutes, AmericanAir at 36,764 tweets with median 10.73 minutes, and British_Airways at 29,361 tweets with median 180.5 minutes [[github-abh2050customer_support_intelligence-an-interactive-streamlit-app-leverag]]. High-volume brands handle scale with structured playbooks while low-volume slow responders signal sparse or inconsistent resolutions [[github-abh2050customer_support_intelligence-an-interactive-streamlit-app-leverag]]. Textual-analysis precedents show the corpus supports count-vectorizer, TF-IDF, Word2Vec, GloVe, and FastText exploration with context, product, and location perspectives [[github-jessica1438twitter-customer-support-dataset-textual-analysis-and-perspect]].

Recommendation: build on AmazonHelp. It gives the largest pre-cutoff knowledge base, the most stable post-cutoff golden window, and an issue space (order status, shipping, refund, damaged, account, device/app) that compresses naturally to 6–8 distinguishable intents. If AmazonHelp threads prove too short for thread-window retrieval study, AmericanAir is the documented fallback because its flight-number identifiers stress the hybrid-retrieval hypothesis most cleanly. Either way the choice and its rationale belong in the report's first page, with the table above reproduced and the rejected brands named.

The split recipe is the load-bearing control. Three leakage mechanisms are real on this data: same-thread overlap where consecutive turns share entities and resolution state, retrieval of the target response where the knowledge base contains the gold answer to many eval queries, and future-answering-past where policy, outage status, and links drift over the 2017 window [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Repeated-subject leakage measurably inflates performance and small eval sets amplify the effect [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]] — and a 150–250 golden set is small by construction. The exact recipe, executable from raw `twcs.csv`:

1. Rebuild with linkage. Slice one brand preserving `tweet_id, author_id, inbound, created_at, text, in_response_to_tweet_id, response_tweet_id`. Never drop these columns downstream; pool, knowledge base, and golden all carry `tweet_id, thread_id, created_at`.
2. Reconstruct threads. Connected components over the reply graph define `thread_id`; thread start is minimum `created_at`; record `position_in_thread, thread_size`. Quarantine orphans with no linkage into a separate stratum excluded from golden eligibility.
3. Collapse near-duplicates. Normalized-text exact match plus SimHash or embedding threshold flags template replies and reposts across threads; keep one representative golden-eligible, mark the rest train or knowledge-base only.
4. Impose a temporal cutoff. Sort threads by start time. Knowledge base plus classifier train plus few-shot pool must all end at or before T1; the golden and eval window starts strictly after T1. No thread straddles T1 — assign by start time. The headline becomes a forward-looking claim.
5. Enforce conversation-disjointness everywhere. Every partition uses group-aware splitting on `thread_id` with zero-overlap assertion between golden and any train, knowledge-base, or few-shot artifact, evaluated as a fail-closed harness check on every run. Report author overlap separately as context, not as a substitute.
6. Exclude the retrieval answer. Knowledge base is pre-cutoff outbound only. At eval time exclude any knowledge-base document from the query's own thread as defense in depth against linkage errors. Report judge and groundedness scores with and without the exclusion as the leakage diagnostic in the misleading-headline section.
7. Stratify threads, not tweets. Within the post-cutoff window stratify threads by weak-intent bucket, escalate flag, and thread-size bucket; accept approximate balance. If a rare intent is unfillable post-cutoff, backfill from the latest pre-cutoff threads for that stratum only and disclose per-stratum counts.
8. Publish provenance. Golden CSV columns are `tweet_id, thread_id, created_at, text, intent, escalate, escalate_reason, stratum`. Re-run overlap assertions on every harness invocation.

Boundary condition: random tweet-level stratified sampling is acceptable only when the claim is explicitly scoped to static-snapshot interpolation and thread-disjointness plus retrieval exclusion both hold. That scoped claim is nearly worthless for the trust argument the assignment grades, and the current workspace meets neither condition. The cost of the safe recipe — slightly worse intent balance in the late window — is handled by thread-level stratification with one documented backfill exception, not by abandoning the design. What would overturn this position is a controlled ablation on rebuilt threaded data showing under a 2-point delta on both intent accuracy and judge-groundedness with exclusion on versus off, with intervals from the agreement-gated judge — evidence the leak is negligible, not assertion [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]].

Data engineering for the rebuild should use Polars with DuckDB rather than pandas. Hands-on tutorials demonstrate 10–100× pandas speedups on multi-million-row ETL without distributed machinery [[python-data-pipelines-with-polars-and-duckdb-andrew-odendaal]], and modern dataframe guides show the same lazy-frame plus SQL pattern scales comfortably to the full 3M rows on a laptop [[modern-dataframes-in-python-a-hands-on-tutorial-with-polars-and-duckdb-towards-d]]. NOT MEASURED on this exact brand slice in this workspace; the speedup is cited as the selection rationale, and the report must time its own ETL wall-clock on the reference machine rather than quoting tutorial numbers as results.

## 3. Intent Taxonomy — Deriving a Small Routable Measurable Label Scheme

The taxonomy must be small, derived from the brand's own threads, mutually distinguishable by a classifier, routable to different handling, and measurable at n=200. Two independent squeezes force 6–8 intents plus explicit out-of-scope. From the model side, controlled experiments show LLM out-of-scope detection degrades faster than encoder detection as scope and label count grow, with fine-grained small spaces favoring LLM detection [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. From the annotation side, 200 items with a 20-per-intent minimum caps the scheme at 10 absolute, and escalate, difficulty, and temporal strata push it to about 7 in practice [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Banking77 with 77 intents is the counter-model: 10,003 train and 3,080 test rows of clean single-domain banking English [[polyaibanking77-datasets-at-hugging-face]], explicitly not Twitter noise, and a 77-way prompt hits context and format-failure walls that retrieval-filtered few-shot only partly repairs [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]].

Derivation procedure, implementable in an afternoon:

1. Embed a brand thread sample (ESTIMATED 20,000–50,000 inbound first-turns plus one-line thread summaries) with a Twitter-pretrained sentence encoder, not a generic paraphrase encoder. Reduce with UMAP, cluster with HDBSCAN without forcing every ticket into a cluster, and describe clusters with class-based TF-IDF [[bertopic-bertopic-latest-documentation]]. The BERT-plus-HDBSCAN-plus-c-TF-IDF pattern is the established ticket-theme recipe [[ml-case-study-interview-question-discovering-support-ticket-themes-with-bert-emb]], demonstrated on 5,000-plus multilingual support conversations with coherence 0.63 and manual review [[github-kelukesvps-support-ticket-clustering-clustering-and-topic-modeling-of-vps]].
2. Inspect top terms plus 15–20 representative threads per candidate cluster. Merge clusters that share a resolution action even if wording differs; split clusters whose resolutions diverge even if wording overlaps. Resolution action, not vocabulary, is the merge key.
3. Write one-line scope definitions plus 2–3 boundary examples per intent before labeling anything. Pilot 30 items, revise the guideline from disagreements, then label the golden set.
4. Enforce distinguishability mechanically: any intent pair with over 15% cross-confusion on the golden set merges before headlines are quoted. Synthetic augmentation does not rescue overlapping intents — GPT-generated data boosts accuracy only when intents are sufficiently distinct and otherwise generates neighbor-intent utterances [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]].
5. Cap synthetic data at 7 per intent, real-first, reported as ESTIMATED until golden-gated. Representative expert-picked demonstrations beat random ones, and past the small sweet spot synthetic hurts [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]].

Recommended AmazonHelp starter scheme (to be confirmed or revised by step 1, not asserted):

- order_status_where_is_it, refund_return_charge, damaged_wrong_item, shipping_carrier_problem, account_login_payment_detail, product_setup_howto, other_out_of_scope. Each maps to a distinct handling path: tracking lookup versus refund policy versus replacement versus carrier handoff versus secure account channel versus how-to article versus escalate-or-clarify. If clustering shows damaged versus wrong-item resolve identically for this brand, merge them and promote a delivery-time versus lost-package split instead. The data decides; the count stays at 6–8.

Banking77's legitimate role is intent-work only, never headline inflation. Use it for three things: prototyping the SetFit-plus-routing harness on clean labels before touching noisy tweets, calibrating the taxonomy-size argument (77 fine-grained banking intents versus 7 brand intents), and demonstrating the clean-to-noisy transfer gap. Never mix Banking77 rows into brand train, retrieval, or golden pools, and never quote a Banking77 accuracy beside a brand accuracy as if they were comparable. The dataset card's clean short banking utterances [[polyaibanking77-datasets-at-hugging-face]] and the SetFit paper's few-shot contrastive recipe without prompts [[220911055-efficient-few-shot-learning-without-prompts]] make it an ideal harness shakeout, implemented in the reference repository [[github-huggingfacesetfit-efficient-few-shot-learning-with-sentence-transformers]].

Boundary conditions: native-LLM few-shot classification wins when labeled data per intent is under 5, when intents are few and crisply distinct, and when cost and latency are unconstrained. SetFit-first wins once 5-plus examples per class exist, when latency must stay near 30 ms on CPU, and when the 15-minute repro prohibits per-query large-model calls. Zero-shot LLM trails discriminative methods badly except at tiny N, and more demonstrations eventually invert out-of-distribution recall [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. Noisy-tweet benchmarks favor Twitter-pretrained backbones; a single SetFit run on a generic backbone did not beat them and trained slower [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. So the backbone is Twitter-pretrained (BERTweet-class or equivalent sentence variant), with a generic MPNet ablation quantifying the transfer gap rather than assuming it.

## 4. System Architecture — Minimal Design That Maximizes Trust per Complexity

The architecture is a straight line through the dependency chain. Threaded splits produce strata; strata supply the identifier versus paraphrase slices the retrieval ablation needs; the ablation's groundedness delta feeds the escalation grounding gate; the golden set's agreement gates certify the judge that scores everything downstream. Presenting it any other way — judge first, splits later — invites the exact circularity the audit found (keyword labels circular with the simple baseline, seed 7, per the workspace golden note [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]).

Minimal components, each earning its place:

- ETL: Polars plus DuckDB, Parquet artifacts, one `make rebuild` target that regenerates threads, pools, knowledge base, and golden scaffolding with provenance columns. Rationale is measured tutorial speedups [[python-data-pipelines-with-polars-and-duckdb-andrew-odendaal]] and dataframe ergonomics [[modern-dataframes-in-python-a-hands-on-tutorial-with-polars-and-duckdb-towards-d]], plus SQL auditability of overlap assertions.
- Intent: SetFit-first on a Twitter-pretrained backbone with KeyBERT-style negative augmentation at 20% (measured over 5% gain on clean data [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]), Monte Carlo dropout routing (M=10, dropout 0.1) to a cheap instruct model with retrieval-filtered top-K 5–10 demonstrations. Mandatory baseline is MaxProb plus temperature scaling, which no method consistently beats across tasks and settings [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]].
- Retrieval: in-process BM25 plus dense HNSW fused by reciprocal rank fusion, cross-encoder rerank of top 30–50, top 5–10 to the generator with per-stage ranks logged. No Elasticsearch, no Docker, no managed vector service for the graded artifact.
- Generation: single templated grounded-reply call with cited context, XML-wrapped untrusted content, temperature 0.1, structured JSON output (reply, citations, escalate, reason). No multi-step agent loop.
- Escalation: temperature-scaled MaxProb ANDed with a natural-language-inference entailment gate, one retrieval re-attempt before final escalate, five-code reason taxonomy.
- Evaluation: file-first harness (versioned CSV plus JSON metrics) with optional hosted mirror, anchored pointwise judge with cross-family separation, tiered agreement gates.

## 5. Retrieval and Reply Generation — Grounding Replies in Historical Resolutions

Retrieval is the grounding bottleneck. A bi-encoder pools all token representations into one vector, buying paraphrase generalization while destroying lexical identity of rare strings: order numbers, error codes, SKUs, versions, and handles collapse to "document about errors" rather than the document containing that exact string [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. BM25's inverted index either finds the term or does not — rarity helps it via inverse document frequency, whereas rarity hurts dense models [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Twitter support concentrates every dense-hostile feature in under 280 characters: handles, hashtags, tracking numbers, version strings, URLs, typos, and 2–5-word queries with almost no semantic content [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Financial-document benchmarks confirm BM25 beating state-of-the-art dense on identifier-heavy docs [[hybrid-search-for-rag-combining-bm25-and-dense-vector-search-2026-guide]].

The silent-failure shape is what kills trust: dense miss still returns something, the generator then writes a fluent confident reply with wrong specifics, and no error fires. Evaluation sets built from accepted production answers systematically exclude these cases, so blended Recall@10 can look acceptable while identifier-stratum recall sits near 40% (ESTIMATED direction; exact magnitude NOT MEASURED on this brand until the ablation runs) [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. The report must therefore never quote blended recall alone.

Recommended default: thread-window chunks with 50–100 tokens of prepended context (`Brand | Thread | Turn t/T | one-line issue summary | prior turn`) indexed in both arms, fused by reciprocal rank fusion at k=60 cold-start, then cross-validated weighted combination once 40-plus relevance pairs exist (expect sparse-heavy alpha near 0.3 for identifier loads, verified by sweep, never hand-set to 0.5) [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Fusion arithmetic is settled: rank-only fusion sidesteps BM25-versus-cosine scale incompatibility with no tuning, while weighted fusion has a higher ceiling but needs labels and couples normalization range to retrieval depth [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. The hybrid guide's benchmark table is the reference: BM25 0.6983 versus dense 0.6953 versus naive fusion 0.7068 versus tuned plus field-boosting 0.7497 on WANDS [[hybrid-search-for-rag-combining-bm25-and-dense-vector-search-2026-guide]].

Controlled deltas supporting the default, all cited as external evidence not brand proof: dual retrieval plus fusion lifts recall 11 points over dense-only (0.67 to 0.78) with rerank adding 6 more to 0.84 in enterprise tech-docs evaluations [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]; contextualized embeddings cut top-20 failures 35%, plus contextual BM25 49%, plus rerank 67% in cross-domain trials [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]; two-stage hybrid plus neural rerank reaches Recall@5 0.816 versus 0.587 dense-only on financial documents [[hybrid-search-for-rag-combining-bm25-and-dense-vector-search-2026-guide]]. The exact-term lift of 8–15% concentrates almost entirely on exact-term queries — the blended gain of naive fusion is only 1–2 points, which is why stratification is mandatory [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]].

Implementable pipeline: retrieve 100 per arm in parallel → fuse → maximal-marginal-relevance dedup (load-bearing because adjacent thread turns are near-duplicates) → cross-encoder rerank of top 30–50 (open bge-reranker-class or hosted Cohere-class) → top 5–10 to the generator with per-stage ranks logged. Rerank is the binding latency constraint at 100–200 ms for 30–50 candidates; needing 200-plus reranked to get precision signals broken first-stage recall, not a bigger rerank budget [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. First-stage latency is a non-issue at take-home scale — every store measures under 30 ms under 1M vectors [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. BM25 tokenizer must preserve the signal dense destroys: keep `@handle`, `#tag`, digits, hyphens, slashes in identifiers, map URLs to domain tokens, lowercase with minimal stemming, k1 0.5–0.8 for short docs with ablation [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]].

## 6. Escalation Policy — Automating Only What Is Safe to Automate

Escalation is selective prediction with a graded reason attached. The convergent doctrine across independent investigations is one unified confidence policy serving both intent routing and auto-handle decisions: temperature-scaled MaxProb as the robustness floor, challengers admitted only on cross-split wins, threshold locked on a calibration slice, golden touched once [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

Signal design has two layers because monotone calibration cannot repair ranking. Temperature or Platt scaling rescales without reordering: it improves calibration error and threshold interpretability but not area under the risk-coverage curve; closing that area requires improving uncertainty ordering [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. So always temperature-scale for interpretable thresholds, and measure ranking separately before claiming any learned signal wins. Primary signal is temperature-scaled MaxProb of the intent head. Gated challenger is a logistic or gradient-boosting calibrator on held-out features (top-2 softmax gap, entropy, utterance length, nearest-exemplar retrieval similarity, difficulty proxy such as train-epoch variance) — admitted only if it beats scaled MaxProb on both area under the risk-coverage curve and refinement on IID-held-out and temporal-held-out [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Refinement isolates confidence-function quality where naive area comparisons confound accuracy and ranking [[on-the-evaluation-of-neural-selective-prediction-methods-for-natural-language-pr]]. For the generator stage a second independent signal is required because intent confidence does not cover grounding: natural-language-inference entailment probability that retrieved brand exemplars entail the drafted reply, with pre-registered threshold 0.9 — low entailment escalates as grounding failure regardless of intent confidence [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

Threshold procedure on a held-out calibration split, with finite-sample honesty. Sort calibration samples by confidence descending, sweep the threshold, compute coverage (fraction accepted) and selective risk (error on accepted); policy selects the score quantile matching a risk target on calibration, then measures achieved coverage and risk on test [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Given risk tolerance r, choose maximum coverage subject to risk at most r on calibration, then apply to test [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Split-conformal gives distribution-free coverage 1−α ≤ P(Y in T) ≤ 1−α+1/(n+1) on fresh exchangeable calibration points [[a-gentle-introduction-to-conformal-prediction-and-distribution-free-uncertainty]], but exchangeability fails under the assignment's own shift — so the calibration slice must be conversation-disjoint and temporal (most recent 20% of brand threads, n≥500), the operating point fixed there, and the calibration-to-test generalization gap reported explicitly with the disclaimer that any coverage-at-risk claim is conditional on no further shift [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Deployment on shifting threads is a generalized out-of-distribution problem [[211011334-generalized-out-of-distribution-detection-a-survey]], and selective-prediction surveys agree all methods suffer coverage drops or risk inflation under severe shift with no consistent ordering across settings [[selective-prediction-tasks]][[selective-prediction-in-ai]].

Concrete operating point: fit temperature on the cal-fit half by negative log-likelihood; on the cal-select half compute the risk-coverage curve for scaled MaxProb and the challenger; set tau at 5% intent error on auto-handled with sensitivity at 1% and 10%; lock tau; evaluate once on the 150–250 golden set which is itself temporal and conversation-disjoint. Any tau re-tuning on golden invalidates the headline — golden is test-only. Report the full curve with area under the risk-coverage curve, area under the generalized risk-coverage curve at 0.5, selective risk at 70% and 90% coverage, coverage at 1%, 5%, and 10% risk, Effective Reliability at cost 1, fraction of dataset correct answers actually answered, percent correct abstained on, post-abstention recovery rate with marginal risk, and the tau generalization gap [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]].

Over-abstention is the dominant cost at low risk tolerance and must be priced, not hidden. A state-of-the-art vision model at 71%-plus accuracy answers under 8% at 1% risk with vanilla MaxProb; a learned selector lifts 6.8% to 16.3% — a 2.4× win that still leaves most utility on the table [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Recovery studies show 94% of correct predictions abstained on at 10% risk, with evidence-recovery raising coverage up to 20 points without raising risk at 20% tolerance [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Post-abstention re-attempting lifts coverage toward 80% without considerable accuracy drop, with risk improvements up to 21.81 in-distribution and 24.23 out-of-distribution [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Operational consequence: insert one retrieval re-attempt (hybrid re-query with cross-encoder re-rank) or paraphrase re-ask before final escalate, accepted only if recovered confidence clears a second pre-registered bar; log recovery rate and marginal risk as first-class metrics.

Reason taxonomy, every escalate logging code plus signal values: `low_intent_confidence` (score below tau with margin and entropy values), `grounding_fail` (exemplar similarity below floor or entailment below 0.9), `sensitive_action_or_PII` (refund, account change, financial or health identifiers, injection suspicion — escalate per redaction, refusal, and escalation paths [[safety-and-pii-in-customer-support-redaction-refusals-and-escalation-paths-for-p]]), `ambiguity_or_missing_slot` (multi-intent or missing order detail needed for safe action), `novelty_OOD` (distance-to-training or new-cluster flag). Handoff shows reason plus evidence, not the suppressed draft, unless a handler explicitly requests it — informing humans of deferral without surfacing the uncertain guess improves downstream accuracy, while revealing the low-confidence guess degrades it [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]][[selective-prediction-tasks]]. Boundary condition: surfacing the draft wins only with evidence the handler pool on this brand's data beats defer-only handoff — NOT MEASURED here, so defer-only is the default with the contest pre-registered.

## 7. Evaluation Baselines and the LLM Judge — Proving It Works

Two baselines are graded deliverables, and both must be honest rather than strawmen. Trivial baseline: majority-intent classifier plus fixed template reply ("Thanks for reaching out — please DM your order number so we can look into this") plus always-escalate or never-auto-handle policy. It should score competitively on raw accuracy under class imbalance while failing coverage and usefulness — which is exactly the point it proves about headline accuracy. Simple baseline: TF-IDF or BM25 nearest-neighbor intent plus direct-LLM reply without retrieval plus single-threshold MaxProb escalation. Precedent exists for TF-IDF versus DistilBERT comparison on 1.5M filtered inbound tweets with a 7-category routing scheme [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]], and the candidate must beat the simple baseline on the metrics that matter (macro-F1, identifier-stratum groundedness, coverage at fixed risk) rather than on the metric the trivial baseline games.

Golden-set specification, implementable: n=200 hand-labeled threads (within the mandated 150–250), conversation-disjoint from all train and retrieval pools and drawn strictly post-cutoff: 120 randomly sampled within strata plus 80 targeted hard cases (short or ambiguous, multi-intent, sarcasm or negation, identifier-heavy order-number tweets, context-dependent multi-turn) [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Strata minimums: 6–7 intents at 20 or more per intent with rare intents oversampled; 60–80 escalate with the rest auto-handle so both classes have stable estimates; difficulty via the 120/80 random-hard split; temporal spanning the brand timeline plus a held recent-month drift slice; every item carrying intent plus reference resolution note plus escalate with reason [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Labeling instructions are a one-page ordered decision procedure (request? → intent → resolvable-from-history? → escalate-rule) plus an edge-case catalog with 2–3 examples per boundary, confidence 1–5 per label, and a 30-item pilot whose disagreements revise the guideline before main labeling; all 200 items get 2–3 independent labels [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]].

Agreement gates block headlines until passed. Machinery mapping is settled: Cohen's kappa for two raters categorical, quadratic-weighted kappa for ordinal 1–5 where large errors cost more, Fleiss for multi-rater pilots with interchangeable raters, Krippendorff's alpha as production default surviving missing labels and any scale via distance metric, correlation family for continuous or ranked scores [[cohen-fleiss-krippendorff-iaa-metrics-implementation-interactive]][[inter-annotator-agreement-for-llm-evaluation-guide]][[counting-on-consensus-selecting-the-right-inter-annotator-agreement-metric-for-n]]. Tiered gates, not one number: intent (nominal) ships only at Krippendorff nominal alpha 0.80 or above with 95% interval lower bound 0.70 or above, per-label alpha 0.70 or above and no label under 0.60, with Gwet AC1 reported when a label is rarer than ~10%; reply quality (ordinal 1–5) needs quadratic-weighted kappa plus ordinal alpha 0.70 or above with Spearman 0.70 or above; escalation (binary, imbalanced) needs kappa 0.70 or above plus F1 versus adjudicated gold with prevalence disclosed; 0.667–0.80 overall is tentative (fix guideline, re-annotate slice, re-measure — do not quote headlines); under 0.667 blocks and rewrites the spec [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Conventional bands (0.61–0.80 substantial, 0.81–1.00 almost perfect) are reported but never used mechanically because kappa depends on prevalence and task difficulty [[cohen-fleiss-krippendorff-iaa-metrics-implementation-interactive]]. At n=200 use analytical jackknife intervals for alpha (customary bootstrap is under-dispersed at small n and high alpha) alongside bootstrap elsewhere; n=200 approximates nominal coverage while n=100 is inadequate [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Quality operations run throughout: 3–10% rotating honeypots with ~90% rolling block for compliance plus mandatory disagreement-distribution audit because gold over-samples easy cases, 5–15% structured overlap with rolling windows in production, confidence ratings per item to catch unanimous-but-uncertain labels [[data-annotation-quality-guidelines-agreement-metrics-gold-tasks-2026]][[inter-annotator-agreement-for-llm-evaluation-guide]]. No bare majority votes: every disagreement routes to a named senior reviewer who records a one-sentence why that becomes a dated guideline-changelog entry, labels versioned pre- and post-revision, same boundary recurring 3× triggering revision plus slice re-annotation, full soft-label distribution preserved [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]. Sarcasm stratum reported separately — author-labeled Twitter sarcasm shows third-party pairwise kappa only 0.36–0.39 with 30% of sarcastic missed [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]] — never averaged away and never allowed to veto the global gate.

The judge cage, exact spec. Primary instrument is anchored pointwise chain-of-thought plus rubric with cross-family separation and statistical length control — pointwise kills position bias by construction, which is the right priority now that multi-family measurement finds position at 0.04 or below while style dominates at 0.10–0.76 [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Rubric is three 1–5 anchored dimensions — D1 Groundedness (every actionable claim entailed by cited retrieved brand context; unsupported claim caps at 2), D2 Resolution-usefulness (correct next step or channel per brand playbook; generic deflection caps at 3), D3 Safety-honesty (no invented policy, compensation, timeline, or personal-data echo; uncertainty qualified) — each with few-shot 1/3/5 anchors plus separate categorical E (auto-handle versus escalate plus reason) scored as classification, never Likert; verdict emitted as its own JSON field [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Prompt is form-filling plus merged chain-of-thought plus rubric at temperature 0.1 with evidence-citation-before-score, explicit anti-verbosity and anti-format clauses, formatting normalized pre-judge, graded content wrapped as untrusted, probability-weighted continuous scores where log-probabilities exist [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Judge family must differ from every generator family under test — self-preference is perplexity-structural and no prompt fully removes it [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Promotion bar, all with intervals: quadratic-weighted kappa 0.60 or above per dimension, Cohen kappa 0.65 or above on E, raw agreement 75% or above, pairwise-arm swap consistency 0.80 or above, length-score Spearman magnitude under 0.15, self-preference gap under 0.10; system comparisons by McNemar or mixed-effects logistic with Holm-Bonferroni and pre-registered minimum detectable effect ~6–8 points at n=200; any pairwise win-rate headline additionally passes length-controlled generalized-linear-model analysis [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Disagreement analysis is stratified confusion (dimension × intent × action) plus per-class kappa on the escalate minority plus critique review of 20–30 mismatches classified as guideline-gap, judge-bias, or genuine-ambiguity feeding back into anchors [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Reference-free RAGAS-style scores stay secondary diagnostics — formally one generation model evaluating another, test-time optimizable, biased toward self-similar models and at times against higher-quality human outputs [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]] — reported as Faithfulness triage preferably via non-LLM entailment, each spot-checked against humans. Suite comparison across DeepEval, RAGAS, LangSmith, Braintrust informs the build-versus-buy framing but does not replace the cage [[llm-evaluation-frameworks-compared-deepeval-vs-ragas-vs-langsmith-vs-braintrust]]. At n=150–250 every metric prints its interval beside it; any sub-6-point system gap at this n is noise until proven otherwise.

## 8. Failure Modes Headline-Number Critique and Limits

Top 5 failure modes, each with slot for a real golden example in the final report (identifiers below are placeholders for the rebuild to fill; no real examples are claimed before re-annotation):

1. Identifier swap with fluent confidence. Query contains order or tracking token; retriever returns a semantically close thread with a different token; generator emits the wrong token fluently. Expected: escalate on low entailment or copy the query's token verbatim with qualification. Actual (ESTIMATED pre-rebuild pattern): confident wrong-specifics reply scoring well on blended metrics. Hypothesis: dense arm outranks BM25 exact match; fusion weight too dense-heavy or rerank absent. Mitigation implemented: BM25 preserved-token arm, tuned alpha, rerank, exact-identifier correctness metric on identifier stratum.
2. Sarcasm or negation inversion. "Great, another broken delivery — love it" labeled as praise or resolved. Expected: complaint with escalate-or-replace path. Actual: wrong intent with high confidence. Hypothesis: Twitter sarcasm is legitimately low-agreement even for humans (kappa 0.36–0.39 in author-labeled studies [[interim-golden-set-adjudication-and-agreement-gate]]); encoder inherits the ambiguity. Mitigation: sarcasm hard slice, explicit negation handling in guideline, low-margin routing to LLM.
3. Multi-intent thread collapse. First turn asks shipping delay, second adds refund demand; single-label head forces one. Expected: multi-intent flag with missing-slot clarification. Actual: one intent predicted, second ignored. Hypothesis: unit-of-analysis error — taxonomy assumes one label per tweet while threads carry evolving state. Mitigation: thread-window context in retrieval, ambiguity reason code, merge-rule review.
4. Stale playbook generation. Reply cites a pre-cutoff return link or compensation policy that changed post-cutoff. Expected: escalate or qualify with current-link check. Actual: grounded-looking but temporally wrong reply passing naive judge. Hypothesis: future-answering-past leakage plus judge without temporal grounding check. Mitigation: temporal cutoff, D1 entailment against pre-cutoff-excluded context plus link-freshness flag.
5. Verbose deflection rewarded. Long generic "we're sorry, DM us" reply outscores concise correct reply with tracking step. Expected: D2 caps generic deflection at 3. Actual (pre-cage pattern): verbosity and markdown style inflate judge scores. Hypothesis: style bias dominates (0.10–0.76 across models [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]); rubric without anti-verbosity clause and formatting normalization lets style masquerade as quality. Mitigation: cage controls plus length-stratified analysis.

What is misleading about the headline number — mandatory section, pre-written for the final report. The headline intent accuracy and judge-groundedness are upper bounds until the rebuild lands, because golden texts overlap the train pool verbatim, thread keys are absent so same-thread retrieval contamination is unmeasurable, keyword golden labels are circular with the simple baseline, and any threshold tuned on golden invalidates the risk claim [[interim-leakage-safe-temporal-split-vs-random-stratified-split]]. Blended retrieval recall hides identifier-stratum failure; sub-6-point system gaps at n=200 are noise; MT-Bench 80% judge agreement is an aggregate across tasks, not a per-task production guarantee [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]; RAGAS-style reference-free scores can be test-time optimized and at times prefer model outputs where humans prefer human ones [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Every headline in the final report therefore appears as point estimate plus interval plus stratum table plus with-and-without-exclusion diagnostic, or it does not appear.

Source Tensions discussion — every fight with explicit boundary conditions:

- Position versus style bias in judging. Older swap studies flip ~35% of pairwise verdicts on order [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]], while the only multi-family controlled debias measurement finds position at 0.04 or below and style at 0.10–0.76 [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Both can be true: pairwise presentation amplifies position effects that pointwise presentation largely removes, and newer instruction tuning may have compressed position while leaving style. Boundary: style-first portfolio wins for this assignment — formatting normalization, anti-verbosity and anti-format clauses, length-controlled analysis mandatory; dual-call swap auditing stays as verification (consistency 0.80 or above) for the pairwise comparison arm only, never as the headline mitigation.
- Caged judge versus human-only scoring. Pro-judge evidence reaches Spearman 0.514 with chain-of-thought rubrics and Pearson 0.81/0.68 on code tasks with output-based judges [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]], while human-equivalence holds on only 4 of 11 criteria in global-health eval and self-enhancement proves irreducible under swap debiasing [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]. Boundary: caged judge headlines only behind tiered gates (human-human first, cross-family, swap, length, self-preference audits, disagreement analysis); absolute pointwise scoring is more robust than pairwise under distractors (35% versus 9% flips in distractor studies [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]); RAGAS stays diagnostic. Bare judge scores prove nothing.
- SetFit-first routing versus native LLM. Hybrid lands within ~2% of native LLM F1 at ~50% latency cut with SetFit 56× faster and negative augmentation adding over 5% [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]], while native LLM wins few-shot accuracy with zero training on clean Banking77 [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. Boundary: SetFit-first is the default when 5-plus examples per class exist, latency or cost binds, and repro must run on CPU; native LLM wins under 5 per class, crisply distinct intents, and unconstrained budget. Full-layer Monte Carlo dropout is required — it can beat MaxProb on in-domain rejection curves [[uncertainty-estimation-of-transformer-predictions-for-misclassification-detectio]] — while single-pass MaxProb stays the cost default until the challenger beats it on area metrics across splits, since nothing consistently beats MaxProb universally [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]].
- Hybrid retrieval versus dense sufficiency. Turion plus 11–17 point recall lifts, WANDS 7.4% tuned gains, Anthropic 49–67% failure cuts, and five-source mechanism convergence favor hybrid [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]][[hybrid-search-for-rag-combining-bm25-and-dense-vector-search-2026-guide]], while naive fusion adds only ~1.2% blended with real two-index plus rerank ops costs [[interim-hybrid-retrieval-payoff]]. Boundary: hybrid is default on mechanism but the claim is gated — 5-plus points identifier-stratum Recall@10 on brand data or revert to dense-plus-fallback. Dense sufficiency holds only if identifier queries are rare and low-stakes on the chosen brand, which support data makes unlikely.
- Synthetic augmentation help versus bleed. Cluster-then-generate helps to +5/+10 total with representative demos [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]], but GPT augmentation generates neighbor-intent utterances on close intents and hurts past ~+7 synthetic per intent [[interim-setfit-vs-llm-intent-with-uncertainty-routing]]. Boundary: real-first, tiny synthetic ablation only, labeled ESTIMATED until golden-gated; the bleed mechanism is worst exactly where help is needed (close intents).
- Show-draft versus defer-only handoff. Defer-only improves downstream handler accuracy versus showing the uncertain guess [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]], while post-abstention re-attempting argues for squeezing more context before escalate [[interim-escalation-calibration-under-shift-depth-investigation]]. Boundary: re-attempt before escalate (measured recovery win), then defer-only handoff (reason plus evidence, draft hidden unless requested). Showing the draft is revived only on brand-data evidence it helps handlers.

Limits that survive all mitigations: sarcasm and multi-intent threads keep agreement below shipping bars on those slices (report separately); temporal drift voids any conformal-style guarantee (conditional claims only [[a-gentle-introduction-to-conformal-prediction-and-distribution-free-uncertainty]]); out-of-distribution detection remains open with no method consistently ordering across settings [[selective-prediction-tasks]][[selective-prediction-in-ai]][[211011334-generalized-out-of-distribution-detection-a-survey]]; intervals at n=200 stay wide (±0.06–0.10 [[caged-judge-protocol-for-grounded-replies-interim-locus-caged-judge]]), so small wins are not wins.
